
ADHD is a [[5.4d Neurodevelopmental Conditions|Neurodevelopmental Condition]] that primarily affects ability to regulate
- attention
- behavior 
- impulse control

Causes:
- Genetic: Often runs in families
- Brain Structure/Function, primarily in the [[Prefrontal cortex|PFC]]
- Environmental factors
