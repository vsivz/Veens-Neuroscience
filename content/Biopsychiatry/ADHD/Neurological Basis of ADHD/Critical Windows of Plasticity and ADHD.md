[[Adolescence]] is typically a period of heightenened [[neuroplasticity|plasticity]]
During this time the [[Prefrontal cortex|PFC]] strengthens its connections with subcortical structures, including the [[Basal Ganglia and ADHD]] and [[Limbic System]], ensuring tight regulation of emotions,rewards and impulses

In [[ADHD]]], this window of plasticity is disrupted by asynchronous development. The PFC is underdeveloped, but the other regions mature regularly. 
This means that emotional responses dominate due to weak control by the PFC.
