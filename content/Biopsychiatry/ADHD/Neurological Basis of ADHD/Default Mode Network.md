This is a network of brain regions active during rest and self-referential thinking. Neurotypical people can easily supress this when necessary, and switch to the [[Task Positive Network]]. However, in [[ADHD]], poor regulation of the DMN can result in "mind-wandering," which interferes with sustained attention.

It also includes the [[Dorsomedial Prefrontal Cortex|mPFC]] and [[Ventromedial Prefrontal Cortex|vmPFC]], and the [[hippocampus]]
