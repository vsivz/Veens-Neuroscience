Cortical thickness peaks in childhood due to the [[neuroplasticity|proliferation of neural connections]]
During [[Adolescence]], the brain undergoes [[synaptic pruning]]

For individuals with [[ADHD]]], cortical thinning in the [[Prefrontal cortex|PFC]] is delayed by about 2-3 years, particularily in the [[Dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex|DLPFC]] and [[Ventromedial Prefrontal Cortex|vmPFC]]

This means that the brain retains an immature structure for longer, leading to inefficient cognitive processes
