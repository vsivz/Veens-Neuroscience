
Biopsychiatry is a branch of [[Psychiatry]] that focuses on understanding [[psychological disorder|psychiatric disorders]] through the lens of [[Biology|biological]] processes

Key aspects include:
- [[neurotransmitter|neurotransmitters]] - how imbalances in brain chemicals like [[serotonin]], [[dopamine]] & [[norepinephrine]] affect mood and behavior
- [[1.4b Brain Structure|brain structure]] - how abnormalities in certain brain regions are associated with specific disorders
