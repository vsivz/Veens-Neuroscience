The [[Neurobiology]] of [[obsessive-compulsive disorder|OCD]] explains how OCD can arise from neurobiological mechanisms



##### CSTC Loop Dysfunction
The [[Corticostriatal-Thalamocortical Circuit|Corticostriatal-Thalamocortical (CSTC) Circuit]] is a core mechanism in the pathophysiology of OCD
Two of its pathways include:
- The Direct Pathway - Excitatory; 
- The Indirect Pathway - Inhibitory; Suppresses inappropriate behaviors

In OCD:
- there is hyperactivity of the direct pathway, leading to excessive [[Thalamus|thalamic]] activity, reinforcing repetitive thoughts and behaviors
- there is hypoactivity of the indirect pathway, reducing inhibitory control over intrusive thoughts and compulsions
