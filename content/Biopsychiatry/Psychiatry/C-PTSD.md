Complex Posttraumatic Stress Disorder is a mental health condition that arises from *prolonged* or *repeated* exposure to traumatic events, often during critical development periods in childhood, or in contexts where escape is not possible. 

It shares many features with traditional [[posttraumatic stress disorder|PTSD]], but includes additional symptoms and challenges. 

###### Traditional PTSD Symptoms
- Re-experiencing: Flashbacks, nightmares, intrusive memories
- Avoidance: Efforts to avoid reminders of the trauma
- Hyperarousal: Increased vigilance, irritability, and difficulty concentrating

###### Additional Features
- Emotional Dysregulation: Difficulty managing intense emotions
- Negative Self-Concept: Deep feelings of shame, guilt, or worthlessness
- Interpersonal Difficulties: Struggles with trust, forming healthy relationships, or feeling detached from others
- Persistent sense of threat: A chronic perception of danger even in safe environments
- Dissociation
- Chronic physical symptoms.
