
This is a rare but serious condition that occurs in children who have experienced severe neglect or abuse, preventing them from forming healthy emotional bonds with their caregivers.

Children with RAD may show the following:
- Emotional Withdrawal: 
	- Rarely seeks comfort when distressed
	- Does not respond to comfort when offered by caregiver
- Lack of Emotional Responsiveness: 
	- Limited Positive Affect
	- Unexplained episodes of irritability, sadness, or fear
- Social difficulties
	- does not develop age-appropriate social relationships
	- often avoids or is indifferent to other people, including caregivers

RAD stems from early experiences of:
- Severe neglect
- repeated changes in primary caregivers, such as in foster care
- Lack of responsive caregiving
