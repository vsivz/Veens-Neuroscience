  - Avoidant/Restrictive Food Intake Disorder

This is an [[5.4d Feeding and Eating Disorders|eating disorder]] characterized by persistent disturbances in eating behaviors.
Unlike other eating disorders, ARFID is not driven by concerns about body-image or weight

###### Key Features
- **Food avoidance or restriction**
	- A person may avoid or restrict certain foods due to:
		- sensory sensitivities
		- fear of adverse consequences (choking, vomiting, or allergic reactions)
		- lack of interest in eating or food
- **Impact on Functioning**
	- Significant weight loss or failure to achieve expected growth
	- Nutritional deficiencies
	- Dependence on supplements or tube feeding
	- Impaired social functioning (trouble eating with others)
- **No body image concerns**
	- Unlike [[anorexia nervosa|anorexia]] or [[bulimia nervosa|bulimia]], individuals with ARFID do not restrict food due to concerns about their weight or shape

###### Common signs & symptoms
- Eating a limited variety of foods, often "safe" or preferred foods
- refusing to try new foods or extreme pickiness
- fear of eating after a choking or vomiting incident
- difficulty eating in social settings or with unfamiliar foods
- fatigue, weakness, or other physical symptoms due to malnutrition

###### Causes
- **Biological Factors**
	- sensory processing differences
	- heightened sensitivity
- **Psychological Factors**
	- [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety disorders]]
	- [[specific phobia|phobias]]
	- past negative experiences with food
