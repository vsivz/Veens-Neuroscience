Brainwaves are patterns of [[Science/Physics11/Electric Circuits/Electric Circuits|electrical]] activity in the [[1.4b Brain Structure|brain]], generated by the synchronized firing of [[neurons]]
These oscillations reflect different states of [[consciousness]], cognition & emotional processing
Brainwaves are categorized based on their frequency, which corresponds to different mental and physiological states

###### Mechanism
Neurons in the brain communicate via electrical impulses

When groups of neurons fire together in rhythmic patterns, they create brainwave oscillations. 

Brainwaves are detected using [[EEG]], measuring voltage fluctuations on the scalp.
