
There are two important theories of <mark class="kv">personality</mark>:
- psychodynamic theories
- humanistic theories

![[personality|clean no-t]]

Today's personality researchers study:
- the basic dimensions of personality
- interaction of persons and environments
	- inherited traits
	- experiences
	- cultural beliefs and values
- [[self-esteem]]
- self-serving bias
- cultural influences on our concepts of self
- the unconscious mind

Psychologists used many ways to study personality:
- [[Sigmund Freud]]'s psychoanalytic theory thought that childhood sexuality and unconscious motivations influence personality
- humanistic theories focused on our inner capacities for growth and self-fulfillment
- Trait theories study characteristic patterns of behavior: traits
- Social-cognitive theories explore the <mark class="ii">interaction</mark> between traits (incl. thinking) and social context
