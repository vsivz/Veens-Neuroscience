Even in random data, we often find patterns, because random sequences don't often look random. 

If one were to flip a coin 50 times, there will likely be streaks of heads and tails. 
In actual random sequences, patterns and streaks occur more often than people expect. 
That also makes it hard for people to generate random-like sequences. When embezzlers try to simulate random digits to specify how much to steal, their nonrandom patterns can alert fraud experts. 

One reason we are so prone to pattern-seeking is that a random, unpredictable world is unsettling. 
Making sense of our world helps us to relieve [[stress]]
