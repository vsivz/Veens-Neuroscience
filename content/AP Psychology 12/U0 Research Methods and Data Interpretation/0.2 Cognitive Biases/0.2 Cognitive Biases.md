A **cognitive bias** is a systematic pattern of deviation from rationality or objective judgment in thinking. 
It occurs when the brain processes information in a way that leads to inaccurate or irrational conclusions. 
These biases are often a result of shortcuts, known as [[heuristics]], that the brain uses to make decisions more quickly or efficiently.

***

Some things seem like commonsense truth only because we so often hear them repeated. 
Mere repetition of statements - whether true or false - makes them easier to process and remember, and thus appear more truthful
