Psychological scientists use the *scientific method*
It's a self-correcting process for evaluating ideas with observation and analysis. 
It puts hunches and plausible-sounding theories to the test. 
If the data supports the theory's predictions, great, if not, the theory gets revised or rejected. 

When researchers submit their work to a scientific journal, [[peer reviewers]] evaluate it. The Journal editor uses these peer reviews to determine whether or not to publish it.
