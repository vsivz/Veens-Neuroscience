>[!psych] theory
>an explanation using an integrated set of principles that organizes observations and predicts behaviors or events. 

By using deeper principles to organize isolated facts, a theory summarizes and simplifies. 

A good theory produces [[hypothesis|hypotheses]]. 

![[hypothesis|clean no-t]]

These predictions specify which results would support the theory and which would disconfirm it.
The [[falsifiable|falsifiablity]] of a hypothesis is a mark of its scientific strength.
