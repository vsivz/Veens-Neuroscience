>[!psych] naturalistic observation
>a [[0.3a Non-Experimental Methods|non-experimental]] technique of observing and recording behavior in naturally occurring situations without trying to manipulate and control the situation

Digital technology has allowed a massive change of scale in these. 
For example, anonymously tapping into the GPS systems of 15 million cell phones have allowed scientists to track how often people in different geological regions obeyed stay-at-home orders during the [[COVID]] pandemic. 

Like the [[case study]], naturalistic observation does not *explain* behavior, but rather *describes* it. 
Nevertheless, descriptions can be revealing. 
For example, we once thought only humans used tools. Naturalistic Observation revealed that chimpanzees sometimes insert a stick in a termite mound and withdraw it, eating the termites on the stick.
