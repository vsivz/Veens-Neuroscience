The wording effect refers to how even small changes in the order of wording questions can make a big difference. 
Researchers attempt to phrase questions in a way that reduces [[social desirability bias]]. 
When conducting a [[survey]], to counter [[self-report bias]] researchers might pair the survey with other means of measuring behaviors.

Examples:

| Garners more approval  | Garners Less Approval |
| ---------------------- | --------------------- |
| "aid to those in need" | "welfare"             |
| "undocumented workers" | "illegal aliens"      |
| "gun safety laws"      | "gun control laws"    |
