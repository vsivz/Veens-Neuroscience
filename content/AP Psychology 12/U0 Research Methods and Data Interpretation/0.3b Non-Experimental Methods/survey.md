>[!psych] survey
>a [[0.3b Non-Experimental Methods|non-experimental]] technique for obtaining the self-reported attitudes or behaviors of a particular group; usually by questioning a [[representative sample|representative]], [[random sample]] of the group

It looks at many cases in less depth, asking people to report their behaviors or opinions. 
But asking questions is tricky.
People may shade their answers in a [[social desirability bias|socially desirable]] direction. 
The answers can often depend on how the question is [[Wording Effect|worded]]
