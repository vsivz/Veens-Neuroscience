When we believe there is a relationship between two things, due to [[confirmation bias]], we are likely to notice and recall instances that confirm our belief. 
The result is an *illusory correlation*.

>[!psych] illusory correlation
>perceiving a relationship where none exists, or perceiving a stronger-than-actual relationship

Illusory correlations can feed an illusion of control that we can personally influence chance events. 

[[regression toward the mean]] also feeds this phenomenon. Extreme results will most likely regress back to the average. 
A team's very poor performance during a game will likely improve during the next. 
This can lead to superstitious thinking. After berating a team for a poorer performance, a coach may think the berating caused an improvement when they improve due to pure chance.
