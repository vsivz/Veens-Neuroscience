>[!psych] scatterplot
>a graphed cluster of dots, each of which represents the values of two [[variable|variables]]. The [[derivative|slope]] of the points suggests the direction of the relationship between the two variables. The amount of scatter suggests the strength of the [[0.4a Correlation|Correlation]]. (little scatter indicates high correlation)

![[Screenshot_20250321_191626.png#invert]]

The three scatterplots above illustrate the range of possible correlations from a *perfect positive* to a *perfect negative*. 
In reality, perfect correlations rarely occur. 
A correlation is *positive* if two sets of scores tend to rise or fall together.
