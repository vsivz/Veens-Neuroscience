
Psychology is a science. 
- Some questions are beyond science (is there life after death?)
- While many other questions can be answered by searching for *scientifically derived* evidence

Regardless of now sensible or wild an idea seems, one should ask if its works. 

To sift reality from fiction requires a scientific attitude: being skeptical, but not cynical, open-minded, but not gullible

There are three components of a scientific attitude:
- **Curiosity**: "Does it work?"
	- When put to the test, can its predictions be confirmed
- **Skepticism**: "How do you know?"
	- A healthy skepticism is not cynical but not gullible
- **Humility**: "That was unexpected, lets explore further."
	- Researchers must be willing to accept being wrong and follow new ideas
