
>[!psych] ingroup bias
>the tendency to favor our own group


Experiments shown that beginning in early childhood, people favor their own group when dividing rewards. Even if the group was created by a coin toss.
This [[0.2 Cognitive Biases|cognitive bias]] appears more as ingroup favoritism than harm to the outgroup.

This is due to our ancestors' need to belong. 
[[evolutionary psychology|evolution]] predisposes us, when encountering strangers, to make instant judgments: friend or foe.
