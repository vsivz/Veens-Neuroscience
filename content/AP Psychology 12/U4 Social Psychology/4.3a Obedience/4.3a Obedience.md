
**How much do we trust authority over what we know is right?**

>[!psych]  Obedience
>complying with an order or a command

![[Milgram's Experiment|Embed]]

In later experiment's, Milgram discovered that obedience was highest when:
- the person giving the orders was <mark class="ii">perceived to be a legitimate authority figure</mark> and was <mark class="vi">close at hand</mark>
- a <mark class="vi">powerful or prestigious institution</mark> supported the authority figure
- the victim was <mark class="vi">depersonalized or at a distance</mark>, even in another room
- there were no <mark class="gc">[[role models|modeling]]</mark> for defiance

The power of obedience was demonstrated in [[Nazi Germany]]. Obedience alone doesn't explain the Holocaust, it was also fueled by a deep anti-semetic ideology.
