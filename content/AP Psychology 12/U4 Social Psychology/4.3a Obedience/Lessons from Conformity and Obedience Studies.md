
**How much power do we have as individuals?**
In Milgram's experiments, participants were torn between kindness and obedience, which obedience usually wins

Milgram did not entrap his teachers by starting with the most extreme voltage. Focusing on the endpoint right from the start would significantly increase hesitation. Milgram used the <mark class="lc">[[The Foot-in-the-Door Phenomenon]]</mark>, by beginning with a small amount and escalating

Great evils often grow out of people's compliance with lesser evils. 
Nazi leaders suspected most German civil servants would resist directly shooting Jews, but found them willing to handle the paperwork of genocide

Milgram found a similar result in his tests. When he asked men to administer the test while someone else did the shocking, <mark class="na">93%</mark> complied. 


<mark class="kv">Social Control</mark> - the power of the situation and <mark class="kv">Personal Control</mark> - the power of the individual interact. 
When feeling pressured, some people react by doing the <mark class="ii">opposite</mark> of what is expected. 
The power of one or two individuals to sway majorities is <mark class="kv">minority influence</mark>. When you are the minority, you are far more likely to sway the majority if you hold firmly to your position.
