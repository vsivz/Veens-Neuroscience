> [!psych] peripheral route of persuasion
>occurs when people are influenced by incidental cues, such as a speaker's attractiveness. 

It uses attention-getting cues to trigger speedy judgments.

When celebrity endorsements influence us, because of the [[halo effect]], that is an example of the Peripheral Route of Persuasion
