We frequently conform to avoid rejection or to gain social approval.
In these cases, we are responding to <mark class="kv">normative social influence</mark>
People are most conforming to social norms in <mark class="lc">[[collectivist]]</mark> cultures. 

>[!psych] normative social influence
>influence resulting from a person's desire to gain approval or avoid disapproval

People are also responsive to <mark class="kv">dynamic norms</mark>, to how norms are changing
