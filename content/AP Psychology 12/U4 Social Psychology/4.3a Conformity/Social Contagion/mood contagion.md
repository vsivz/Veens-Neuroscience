Social contagion affects <mark class="vi">emotions</mark>. 
We tend to take on the emotional tones of those around us:
- expressions
- postures
- voice tones
-  grammar

Hearing someone read a neutral text in a happy or sad voice creates a <mark class="kv">mood contagion</mark> in others

This natural mimcry helps us to <mark class="kv">empathize</mark> - to feel what others are feeling. 
This explains why we feel happier around happy people than around depressed people. 

It also explains why groups of workers revealed <mark class="kv">mood linkage</mark> - the sharing of moods

We tend to mimic those whom we like and to like those who mimic us
