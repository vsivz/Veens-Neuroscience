**How does culture affect our behavior**
>[!psych] culture
>the enduring behaviors, ideas, attitudes, values and traditions shared by a group of people and transmitted from one generation to the next

Social living, imitation, and language have enabled the *preservation of labor* 
culture also enables *division of labor*

Culture influences how we sleep, eat, dress and learn
for example, in <mark class="lc">[[collectivist]]</mark> cultures, situatins focus on "we", meeting group standards and accomodating others
In <mark class="lc">[[individualist]]</mark> cultures, there is a bigger focus on only "me"
