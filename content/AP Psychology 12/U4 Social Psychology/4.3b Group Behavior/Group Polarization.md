**how does extremism spread**
>[!psych] group polarization
>the enhancement of a group's prevailing inclinations through discussion within the group

It can have beneficial results
progressive students can become more accepting when discussing racial issues

It can have negative effects
right-wing, racist, and fascist students can amplify each other's uneducated principles when discussing racial issues

Analyses of actual terrorist and "terrorist" organizations across the world reveals a mentality that emerges slowly among those who share a grievance.
As susceptible individuals interact in isolation, their views grow more extreme relative to the mean.
They begin to categorize, or recognize, the world as "us" against "them". 

The internet is an easily accessible medium for group polarization.
It connects like-minded people.
These connections can be very useful, for example bringing emotional healing to cancer survivors
These connections can also strengthen social movements
These can be good in cases like:
- Antifa
- Black Lives Matter
Or they can be bad in cases like:
- Climate-Change Denialism
- Vaccine Denialism
- Many right-wing movements and ideologies

>[!pers] Personal Connection
>I have noticed that group polarization tends to be more extreme for right-wing groups, for a few possible reasons:
>- The emotional arguments of the right are much easier for our brains to amplify than calculated left-wing logic
>- Platforms like Meta and Twitter that control the algorithms propogating this content have a profit motive to favor right-wing views
>- Especially in the actual left (not neoliberalism), the true complexity of the arguments being made leaves significant room for discussion and argument, decreasing amplification
