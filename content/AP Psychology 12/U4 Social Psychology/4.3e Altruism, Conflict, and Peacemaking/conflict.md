
>[!psych] conflict
>a perceived incompatibility of actions, goals, or ideas

We can end up harming our collective being by pursuing our personal interests, in a [[social trap]]

![[social trap|clean no-t]]

Those in conflict have a tendency to form diabolical images of each other - [[mirror-image perceptions]]

![[mirror-image perceptions|clean no-t]]

Perceptions can become [[self-fulfilling prophecy]], beliefs confirming themselves by influencing one side to react in ways that seem to justify those beliefs

![[self-fulfilling prophecy|clean no-t]]

**Promoting Peace**

Contact, Cooperation, Communication help to make peace

Negative contact increases disliking
But positive contact, especially *noncompetitive* contact between parties of *equal status*, such as fellow store clerks, typically helps

Cooperation helps
*cooperative* contact helps, when there are [[superordinate goals]] that have to be worked together on
![[superordinate goals|clean no-t]]

industrial-organizational psychologists apply psychology's principles in the workplace, and they may help to facilitate cooperation there
