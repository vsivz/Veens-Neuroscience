
>[!psych] Developmental Psychology
>examines our <mark class="ii">physical, cognitive and social-emotional</mark> development across our lifespan to understand <mark class="vi">how we've become our current selves</mark> and <mark class="vi">how we may change</mark>

 
***
 
Developmental Psychologists study both
- The <mark class="vi">chronological order</mark> of this development
- The <mark class="vi">key themes</mark> of this development

They often use <mark class="gc">Cross-Sectional Studies</mark> (comparing people of different ages) and <mark class="gc">Longitudinal Studies</mark> (comparing people across time)

There are Three Major Dialectics:
- <mark class="ls">Nature vs. Nurture</mark> | How do our <mark class="ii">genes</mark> interact with our <mark class="ii">environment</mark> to influence our development?
- <mark class="ls">Continuity vs. Stages</mark> | What development is <mark class="ii">gradual</mark> and what <mark class="ii">changes abruptly</mark> in separate stages?
- <mark class="ls">Stability vs. Change</mark> | What traits <mark class="ii">persist</mark> and what <mark class="ii">changes</mark>?
