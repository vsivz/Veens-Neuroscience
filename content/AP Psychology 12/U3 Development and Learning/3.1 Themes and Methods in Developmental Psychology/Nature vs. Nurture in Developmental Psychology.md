[[Nature vs. Nurture]] is a key theme in Developmental Psychology 
A <mark class="vi">unique gene combination</mark> created at conception helped to form us as individuals
But <mark class="vi">our experiences also shape us</mark>
Family and Peer relationships teach us how to think and act. 
Even differences initiated by our nature may be amplified by our nurture. 
Modern Psychology views our development as the result of the <mark class="ii">interaction</mark> between these two forces

Regardless of our differences, our <mark class="ii">our shared biology</mark> gives us <mark class="vi">many similarities</mark>
- We share the same life cycle
- We speak to infants in the same ways, and respond in the same ways to their coos and cries

To the extent that family structure, peer influences and parental education predict behavior in one group, they do so for others
