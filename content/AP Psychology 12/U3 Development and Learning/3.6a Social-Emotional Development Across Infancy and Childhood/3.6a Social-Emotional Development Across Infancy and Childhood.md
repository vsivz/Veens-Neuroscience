We are <mark class="ii">social animals</mark>
<mark class="kv">Ecological Systems Theory</mark> argues that different environments we encounter affect our <mark class="vi">cognitive, social and biological development</mark>
It uses <mark class="na">5 nested systems</mark>, ranging from direct to indirect influences:

>[!psych] Chronosystem
>- Life Stage and Related events
>- Ex. Moving, Recession
>>[!psych] Macrosystem
>>- Cultural Influences
>>- Values, Beliefs, economic and political systems
>>>[!psych] Exosystem
>>>- Environments that indirectly Affect a Person
>>>- Ex. A parent's workplace
>>>>[!psych] Mesosystem
>>>>- Relationships between microsystem groups
>>>>- Ex. Parents and Peers
>>>>>[!psych] Microsystem
>>>>>- Immediate, direct-contact groups
>>>>>- Ex. Home, school, community
