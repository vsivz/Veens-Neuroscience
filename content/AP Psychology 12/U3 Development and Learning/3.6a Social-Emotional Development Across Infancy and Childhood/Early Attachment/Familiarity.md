Familiarity is a key to attachment.
In many animals, attchments based on familiarity form during a <mark class="kv">critical period</mark> - an optimal period when certain events must take place to facilitate proper development.
Humans seem to have a critical period for language.

![[imprinting|clean no-it]]

Children do not imprint
However, they do become attached, during a <mark class="ii">less precisely defined</mark> <mark class="kv">sensitive period</mark>
<mark class="vi">Mere exposure</mark> to people and things fosters fondness. 
Children like to reread the same books, rewatch the same movies, and reenact family traditions. 
Familiarity is a safety signal.
