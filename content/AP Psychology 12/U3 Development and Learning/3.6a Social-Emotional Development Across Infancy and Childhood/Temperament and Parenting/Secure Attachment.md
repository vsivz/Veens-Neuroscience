>[!psych] Secure Attachment
>demonstrated by infants who comfortably explore environments in the presence of their caregiver, show only temporary distress when the caregiver leaves, and find comfort in the caregiver's return

Aimsworth and others found that <mark class="vi">sensitive, responsive mothers</mark>, those who noticed what their babies were doing and responded appropriately, had infants who exhibited secure attachment.
