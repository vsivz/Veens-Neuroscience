Other infants show <mark class="kv">insecure attachment</mark> marked by <mark class="vi">[[Anxious Attachment|anxiety]]</mark> or <mark class="vi">[[Avoidant Attachment|avoidance]]</mark> of trusting relationships.
These infants are <mark class="ii">less likely to explore</mark> their surroundings; They may even cling to their mother
When she leaves they either cry loudly and remain upset, or seem indifferent to her departure and return.
Some show <mark class="ii">[[Disorganized Attachment|no consistent behavior]]</mark>

Insenstive, unresponsive mothers, who attended to their babies <mark class="vi">when they felt like it</mark>, but ignored them at other times, often had infants who were insecurely attatched. 

There are <mark class="na">three types</mark> of insecure attachment.
