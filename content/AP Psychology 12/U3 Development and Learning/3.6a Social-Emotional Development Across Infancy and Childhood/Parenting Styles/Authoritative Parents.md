These parents are <mark class="ii">confrontive</mark>
- <mark class="vi">both</mark> demanding and responsive
- exert control by setting rules
- but, especially with older children, <mark class="vi">encourage open discussion</mark>, and <mark class="ii">allow exceptions</mark>

Children of these parents tend to:
- have the highest [[self-esteem]]
- have the highest self-reliance
- have the highest self-regulation
- have the highest helpfulness

Generally recognized as usually the best parenting style
