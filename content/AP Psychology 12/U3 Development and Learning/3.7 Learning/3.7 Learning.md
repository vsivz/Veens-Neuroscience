As humans, we learn from experience

By *learning*, we adapt to our environments. 
We learn to expect and prepare for significant events
We learn to repeat acts that bring rewards, and avoid those that bring punishment
We learn by observing events and people

One way we learn is by *association*. Our mind naturally connects events that occur in sequence.
Learned associations feed habitual behaviors. Habits form when we repeat behaviors in a given context.
As the behavior gets linked with the context, our next experience of that context will evoke our habitual response, especially when *mentally fatigued*

On average, behaviors become habitual after about *66 days*
