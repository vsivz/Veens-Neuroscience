>[!psych] associative learning
>learning that certain events occur together

This process of learning associations is *conditioning*. There are two main forms.

- [[3.7 classical conditioning|classical conditioning]]
We learn to associate two [[stimulus|stimuli]], and thus to anticipate events.
We associate stimuli that *we do not control*, and we respond automatically, exhibiting [[respondent behavior]]

- [[3.8 Operant Conditioning|Operant Conditioning]]
We learn to associate a response (our behavior), and its consequence
We learn to repeat acts that are followed by good results, and avoid those followed by bad results
These associations produce [[operant behavior]]
