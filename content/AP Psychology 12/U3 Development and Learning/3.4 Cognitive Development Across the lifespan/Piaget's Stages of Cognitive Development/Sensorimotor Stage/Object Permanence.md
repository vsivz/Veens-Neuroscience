Before the age of <mark class="na">6 months</mark>, infants lack <mark class="kv">Object Permanence</mark>
By <mark class="na">8 months</mark> they begin exhibiting memory for things no longer seen: if you hide a toy, the child will momentarily look for it. Within another <mark class="na">month or two</mark>, the infant will look for it even after being restrained for several seconds

>[!psych] Object Permanence
> the awareness that things continue to exist even when not perceived
> ^ObjectPermanence

Modern Psychology views object permanance, and development in general, as more continuous than Piaget did.
