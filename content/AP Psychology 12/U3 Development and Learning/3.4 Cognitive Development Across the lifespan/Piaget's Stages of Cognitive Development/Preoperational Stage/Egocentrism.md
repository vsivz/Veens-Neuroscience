Piaget taught us that preschool children are <mark class="kv">egocentric</mark>
>[!psych] egocentrism
>in Piaget's theory, the preoperational child's difficulty taking another's point of view
>^egocentrism

Ex.
- children will block your view of the TV, assuming you can see what they can see
- "How do you get to the other side?" (of the river, which the child is standing on) "You're on the other side"

Even <mark class="lc">[[Adolescence|adolescents]]</mark> egocentrically overestimate the extent to which others share their opinions, knowledge, and perspectives
