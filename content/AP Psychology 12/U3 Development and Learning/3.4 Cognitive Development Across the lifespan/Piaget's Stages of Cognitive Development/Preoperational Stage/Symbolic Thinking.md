<mark class="ii">Symbolic Thinking</mark> and the <mark class="kv">pretend play</mark> it enables appear at an earlier age than Piaget supposed

>[!experiment] 
>Judy DeLoache showed children a model of a room and hid a miniature stuffed dog behind its miniature couch
><mark class="na">2.5 year olds</mark> easily remembered where to find the miniature toy, but could not use the model to locate the actual stuffed dog behind a couch IRL
>6 months later, at <mark class="na">age 3</mark>, children usually went right to the actual stuffed animal in the real room, showing they could think of the model as a <mark class="vi">symbol</mark>

Similarily, <mark class="na">2.5-year-olds</mark> tend to engage in <mark class="kv">parallel play</mark>, playing next to other children but do not try to influence others.
