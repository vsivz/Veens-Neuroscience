Psychologist <mark class="hv">Jonathan Haidt</mark> believes that mcuh of our morality is rooted in <mark class="kv">moral intuitions</mark>: quick, gut feelings.
According to this <mark class="kv">intuitionist</mark> view:
- The mind makes moral judgements quickly and automatically
- Feelings of disgust or of elation trigger moral reasoning

laboratory games reveal that the desire to punish wrongdoing is mostl driven not by reason, but rather by emotional reactions, such as <mark class="ii">moral outrage</mark> and the <mark class="ii">pleasure of revenge</mark>

After the emotional reaction, our conscious, moral reasoning steps in to convince us and others' of the logic that we have felt.
