When adolescents achieve <mark class="lc">[[Formal Operational Stage|formal operations]]</mark>, they apply their new abstract reasoning capacity to the world. 
They may:
- think about what is ideally possible
- compare that ideal to the imperfect nature of the current society
- debate human nature, good and evil, truth and justice

Their sense of fair changes from equality to <mark class="ii">equity</mark>

Reasoning hypothetically and deducing consequences also enable adolescents to detect inconsistencies and spot <mark class="ii">hypocrisy</mark> in others' reasoning
This sometimes leads to heated family arguments
