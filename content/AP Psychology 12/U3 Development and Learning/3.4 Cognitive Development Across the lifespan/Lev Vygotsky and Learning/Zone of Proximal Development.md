Effective mentoring occurs when children are developmentally ready to learn a new skill

For Vygotsky, the <mark class="kv">zone of proximal development</mark> is the zone between what a child can and can't do:
what a child can do <mark class="vi">with help</mark>

Children learn best when their social environment presents them with something in the sweet spot <mark class="ii">between too easy and too difficult</mark>
