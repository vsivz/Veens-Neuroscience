**How do children learn?**
[[USSR|Soviet]] Scientist <mark class="hv">Lev Vygotsky</mark> studied how children think and learn
While <mark class="lc">[[Piaget's Stages of Cognitive Development|Piaget]]</mark> emphasized how the child's mind develops through interaction with the <mark class="ls">physical</mark> environment, Vygotsky emphasized how the mind grows through interaction with the <mark class="ls">socio-cultural environment</mark>

<mark class="lc">[[language]]</mark> provides the building blocks for thinking
By <mark class="na">age 7</mark>, children increasingly think in words and use words to solve problems
Vygotsky said that they do this by internalizing their culture's language and relying on <mark class="vi">inner speech</mark>

Parents who verbally say 'No' when, for ex., pulling their child's hands away from a cup of hot coffee are giving the child a self-control tool

Whether out loud or inaudibly, talking to themselves helps children control their behavior and emotions and master new skills
