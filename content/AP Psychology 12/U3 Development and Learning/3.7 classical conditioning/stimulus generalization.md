>[!psych] stimulus generalization
>in [[3.7 classical conditioning|classical conditioning]], the tendency, once a response has been conditioned, for [[stimulus|stimuli]] similar to the [[conditioned stimulus]] to elicit similar responses
>(In [[3.8 Operant Conditioning|Operant Conditioning]], when responses learned in one situation occur in other, similar situations)

[[Ivan Pavlov|Pavlov]] and his team noticed that a dog conditioned to the sound of one tone also responded to a different tone. 

Generalization can be adaptive, as when toddlers learn to fear moving cars, and then learn to fear moving trucks and motorcycles

But it can be maladaptive, when fear reactions generalize
