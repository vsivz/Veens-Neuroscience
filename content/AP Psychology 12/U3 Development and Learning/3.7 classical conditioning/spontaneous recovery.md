>[!psych] spontaneous recovery
>the reappearance, after a pause, of a weakened [[conditioned response]]

Following [[extinction]], and then a several hours delay, [[Ivan Pavlov|Pavlov]] sounded the tone again, and the dogs drooled in response. 
This suggested that extinction was suppressing the CR, rather than truly eliminating it
