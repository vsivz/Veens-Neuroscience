>[!psych] behaviorism
>the view that psychology
>(1) should be an objective science that 
>(2) studies behavior without reference to mental processes
>Most research psychologists today agree with (1) but not (2)

John B. Watson pioneered this field, and was joined by [[Ivan Pavlov]]
They both began to share a disdain for 'mentalistic' concepts, like consciousness
