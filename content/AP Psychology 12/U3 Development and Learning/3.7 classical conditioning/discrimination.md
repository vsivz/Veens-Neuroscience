>[!psych] discrimination
>in [[3.7 classical conditioning|classical conditioning]], the learned ability to distinguish between a [[conditioned stimulus]] and other [[stimulus|stimuli]] that have not been associated with a conditioned stimulus.
>(In [[3.8 Operant Conditioning|Operant Conditioning]], the ability to distinguish responses that are [[reinforcement|reinforced]] from similar responses that are not reinforced)


Slightly different stimuli can cause vastly different consequences:
After eating a poisonous butterfly, birds will [[stimulus generalization|generalize]], avoiding similar butterflies.
But they will also *discriminate* such butterflies from other edible species
