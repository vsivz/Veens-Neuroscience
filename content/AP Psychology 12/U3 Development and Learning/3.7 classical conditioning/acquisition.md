>[!psych] acquisition
>in [[3.7 classical conditioning|classical conditioning]], the initial stage - when one links a [[neutral stimulus]] and an [[unconditioned stimulus]] so that the neutral stimulus begins triggering the [[conditioned response]].
>(In [[3.8 Operant Conditioning|Operant Conditioning]], the strengthening of a reinforced response)n

Not much time should elapse between presenting the NS and the UCS, $\frac{1}{2}$ second usually works well

If the UCS appears before the NS, conditioning is unlikely.
Classical conditioning is *biologically adaptive*, as it helps prepare for good or bad events. The originally neutral tone became a [[conditioned stimulus|CS]] after signaling an important biological event, the arrival of food (UCS)
