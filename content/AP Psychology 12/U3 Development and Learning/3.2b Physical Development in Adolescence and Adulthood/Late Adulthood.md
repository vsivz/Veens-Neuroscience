Across 1950 to 2015, worldwide life expectancy at birth increased from <mark class="na">50 to 73 years</mark>
This increasing life expectancy combined with decreased birthrates means that older adults are a <mark class="ii">growing population segment</mark>

Throughout lifespan, [[Male-Female Differences|males are more prone to dying]]
<mark class="na">126</mark> male <mark class="lc">[[Prenatal Development|embryos]]</mark> begin life for every <mark class="na">100 females</mark>
But the sex ratio at birth is already <mark class="na">105 males</mark> for every <mark class="na">100 females</mark>
During the <mark class="na">first year</mark>, male infants' death rates are <mark class="na">25%</mark> higher than females
Worldwide, women outlive men by <mark class="na">4.7 years</mark>
By <mark class="na">age 100</mark>, women outnumber men <mark class="na">5 to 1</mark>

Chronic anger and <mark class="gc">[[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]]</mark> increase our risk of premature death
In contrast, low stress and good health habits enable longevity
Researchers have even observed an intriguing <mark class="kv">death-deferral</mark> phenomenon
Across a 15-year period, <mark class="na">2000 to 3000 more Americans</mark> died on the 2 days after Christmas than on Christmas and the 2 days before it
The death rate also increases when people reach their birthdays, and when they survive until after other milestones

### Sersory Abilities, Strength and Stamina
Although physical decline begins in early adulthood, we are not usually acutely aware of it until later in life

With age, visual sharpness diminishes, as does <mark class="gc">[[distance perception]]</mark> and adaptation to light level-changes
The eye's <mark class="gc">[[vision|pupil]]</mark> shrinks and its <mark class="gc">lens</mark> becomes less transperant, reducing the amount of light reaching the <mark class="gc">retina</mark>
A 65-year-old retina receives about <mark class="na">$1 \over 3$</mark> as much light as a 20-year-old one

Senses of smell, hearing, and touch also diminish
In Wales, teens' hanging out around convenience stores have been discouraged by a device that emits an aversive high-pitched sound no one over 30 can hear

### Health

The body's [[immune system]] weakens 
This makes older adults more susceptible to life-threatening ailments

The good news is thanks partly to an accumulation of antibodies, people over 65 suffer few short-term ailments, such as common flu and colds

### The Aging Brain

Up to the <mark class="na">teen years</mark>, we process information with greater and greater speed
Older people take a bit more time to react, to solve perceptual puzzles, and even to remember names
At video games, younger adults are significantly better than older ones
This processing lag can have deadly consequences:
Fatal accident rates per mile driven increase sharply after <mark class="na">75</mark>
By <mark class="na">age 85</mark>, they exceed the 16 year old level

Brain regions important to memory begin to atrophy during aging.
The blood brain barrier breaks down, beginning in the <mark class="gc">[[hippocampus]]</mark>, which furthers cognitive decline
In early adulthood, a small, gradual net loss of brain cells begins, contributing by 80 to a brain-weight reduction of <mark class="na">5%</mark>
Some teenage impulsiveness returns, as the <mark class="gc">[[prefrontal cortex]]</mark> begins to atrophy

However, the aging brain maintains some <mark class="gc">[[neuroplasticity]]</mark>
