**How much do we change after Adolescence?**
The unfolding our life <mark class="vi">continues</mark> across the lifespan
It is more difficult to generalize about adulthood stages than earlier ones
If you know someone is a 1-year-old or 10-year-old, you could say a great deal about each case
But with adults, there are many more similarities among much greater age differences

Yet our life courses are in some ways similar. Physically, cognitively, and especially socially, we differ significantly at, for ex., age 25 than age 50

So we recognize the following terms:
- <mark class="ls">Emerging Adulthood</mark>: age <mark class="na">18</mark> to <mark class="na">mid-twenties</mark>
- <mark class="ls">Early Adulthood</mark>: rougly <mark class="na">twenties</mark> and <mark class="na">thirties</mark>
- <mark class="ls">Middle Adulthood</mark>: to age <mark class="na">65</mark>
- <mark class="ls">Late Adulthood</mark>: the years after <mark class="na">65</mark>

Our physical abilities $\begin{cases} \text{muscular strength}\\ \text{reaction time} \\ \text{mobility, flexibility} \\ \text{cardiac output}\end{cases}$, all begin a almost imperceptible decline in our <mark class="na">mid-twenties</mark>
