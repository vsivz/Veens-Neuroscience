He does not view the cluster as a sexual orientation center. Rather he believes it is an important part of a brain pathway engaged in sexual behavior.

Straight men's brains only showed a response to a female hormone sample. 
In a similar study, gay women's responses differed from those of straight women.

## Genetic Influence
Studies indicate that <mark class="na">about a third</mark> of variation in sexual orientation is attributable to <mark class="gc">[[genes]]</mark>
A same sex orientation does tend to run in families. 
identical twins are somewhat more likely than fraternal twins to share a same-sex orientation
But because sexual orientations differ in many identical twin pairs, especially female twins, we know that its not just genes
This includes <mark class="gc">[[epigenetics|epigenetic markers]]</mark> that help distinguish between straight and gay twins

by altering a single gene in fruit flies, researchers have changed the flies' sexual orientation and behavior
Researchers have found links between sexual orientation and two genes on <mark class="lc">[[Prenatal Sexual Development|chromosomes]]</mark> 13 and 14. 
The first of these influences a brain area that varies in size with sexual orientation
The second incluences thyroid function, which has also been associated with sexual orientation

But human traits are influenced by <mark class="vi">many genes with small effect</mark>
Same-sex behavior is also influenced by many other genes

Researches speculate why genes like these may exist
One possible answer is <mark class="kv">kin selection</mark>
Many of our genes also reside in biological relatives.
Perhaps gay people's genes live on through their supporting the survivial and reproductive success of their relatives

A <mark class="kv">fertile females theory</mark> suggests that the answer may be from maternal genetics
Gay men tend to have more gay relatives on their mother's side than on their father's side
Gay men's heterosexual maternal relatives tend to produce more offspring than do the maternal relatives of straight men
Perhaps the genes that dispose some women to have more children also dispose some men to be attracted to men
This would offset the decreased reproduction of gay men by the increased reproduction of maternal relatives

## Prenatal influences
For sexual orientation the <mark class="lc">[[Prenatal Development|Prenatal Environment]]</mark> also matters. 
In humans, a critical period for fetal brain development seems to be in the second trimester.
Exposure to the hormone levels typically experienced by female fetuses during this period may predispose females to later be attracted to men
Female fetuses most exposed to <mark class="lc">[[Hormones and Sexual Behavior|testosterone]]</mark> may be more likely to later exhibit gender-atypical traits and to experience same-sex desires

The mother's [[immune system]] may also play a role
Men with older brothers have been somewhat more likely to be gay
About <mark class="na">$1 \over 3$</mark> more likely for each older brother
This is called the <mark class="kv">older brother effect</mark>, or the <mark class="kv">fraternal birth order effect</mark>
The reason for this is unclea, but the explanation does seem biological because this does not occur among adopted brothers
One explanation is that male fetuses may stimulate the mother's immune system to produce antibodies, and after each pregnancy with a male fetus, the maternal antibodies may become stronger and may prevend a subsequent male fetus' brain from developing in a male-typical pattern
