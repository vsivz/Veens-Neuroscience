# Overview
**What is Sexual Orientation?**

We express the direction of our sexual interest through our <mark class="kv">sexual orientation</mark>
Our sexual and emotional attractions may be
- female-male (heterosexual orietation)
- to our own sex (same-sex orientation)
- to males and females (bisexual orientation)
- to noone (asexual orientation)

Cultures vary in their attitudes toward same-sex attractions
Women and younger, <mark class="vi">more educated</mark>, adults are more accepting
Regardless of whether a culture is accepting or not, heterosexuality is the most common, and same-sex + other variations exist
In nations where same-sex relationships are illegal, the ratio of gay and bisexual people <mark class="vi">is no different</mark> than in the rest of the world

Surveys in Europe and in the United States show that <mark class="na">~3-4%</mark> of men and <mark class="na">2%</mark> of women have exclusively same-sex attractions
But percentages vary over time, with the percentage who feel comforable self-reporting as gay or bisexual gradually increasing with increased social acceptance
Percentages are also slightly higher when self-reporting is anonymous

In less tolerant places, queer people may try and conceal their orientation, which can harm their <mark class="gc">[[Mental Health]]</mark>
In surveys of US high schoolers, gay and lesbian youth have been <mark class="na">doubly likely</mark> as straight youth to report being bullied
They have also been <mark class="na">3.6 times as likely</mark> to report "persistent feelings of sadness or hopelessness in the past 12 months"
They have been <mark class="na">4.5 times as likely</mark> to have seriously considered attempting <mark class="gc">[[suicide]]</mark>
Some may try to change their sexual orientation through psychotherapy, willpower, or prayer

Modern psychology views sexual orientation as neither willfully chosen nor willfully changed
<mark class="vi">There is no sound evidence that sexual orientation can be changed</mark>
Efforts to do are not successful and involve risk of harm

Women's sexual orientation tends to be less strongly felt and, for some women, is more fluid and changing
Men's sexual drive and interests have been less flexible and varying than have women's
Wome more often prefer to alternate periods of high sexual activity with periods of almost none
