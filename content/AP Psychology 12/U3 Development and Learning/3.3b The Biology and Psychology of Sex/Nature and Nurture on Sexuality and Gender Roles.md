**How does Nurture and Nature influence Sexuality and Gender Roles** 

Nature selects behaviors that <mark class="ii">increase reproductive success</mark>
<mark class="gc">[[evolutionary psychology]]</mark> suggests that <mark class="qu">men pair widely, and women pair wisely</mark>

Women have more at stake
A woman conceives and protects a fetus growing inside her body for up to 9 months, and may nurse for months following birth.
This is why straight women prefer partners who will offer their joint support and protection.

Men tend to prefer traits, such as smooth skin, and a youthful shape, that <mark class="ii">convey health and fertility</mark>
Mating with such women would increase men's chances of propogating their genes

<mark class="gt">[[Nature vs. Nurture]]</mark>
We are designed to prefer whatever worked for our ancestors in their environments
But there is a genetic component too:
genes and hormones may predispose males to be more physically agressive
But culture may <mark class="vi">amplify</mark> this gender differecne through <mark class="vi">norms</mark> that reward dominant men and gentle women

But in many modern cultures, gender roles are merging

Research has found smaller behavior differences between women and men in cultures with greater gender equality

<mark class="lc">[[Social Learning Theory]]</mark> offers an explanation of gender differences. 
We all learn social scripts. 
By <mark class="ii">watching and imitating</mark> others, people may learn certain behaviors pertaining to their own gender roles
