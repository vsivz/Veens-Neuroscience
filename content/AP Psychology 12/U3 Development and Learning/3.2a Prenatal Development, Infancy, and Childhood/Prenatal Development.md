**What is the course of Prenatal Development?**


## Conception
The process starts in one's grandmother- as an egg forming inside a fetus inside her
A woman is born with all the immature eggs whe would ever have
A man starts producing sperm cells at puberty, initially at a rate of <mark class="na">$1000 \over second$</mark>

An ovary releases a mature egg. <mark class="na">~250 million sperm</mark> begin a race upstream to the egg cell. 
The small number that reach the cell release digestive enzymes that ate away the egg's protective coating. 

As soon as one reaches the egg, the surface blocks out all others. 
Within <mark class="na">${1 \over 2}$ a day</mark>, the egg nucleus and sperm fused


## Prenatal Development

The initial <mark class="kv">zygote</mark> begins <mark class="vi">cell division</mark>, until there are <mark class="na">~100</mark> identical cells within the <mark class="na">first week</mark>
<mark class="na">$\leq {1 \over 2}$</mark> of zygotes survive beyond the first 2 weeks.
After the first week, cells begin to <mark class="ii">differentiate</mark> in structure and function

<mark class="na">~10 Days</mark> after conception, the <mark class="kv">germinal stage</mark> completes  as the zygote attatches to the mother's uterine wall

Then, the <mark class="kv">embryonic stage</mark> begins. 
The <mark class="ii">inner cells</mark>  become the <mark class="kv">embryo</mark> 
many of the <mark class="ii">outer cells</mark> become the <mark class="kv">placenta</mark>, a <mark class="ii">life-link that tranfers nutrients and oxygen to the embryo</mark> 

<mark class="na">9 weeks</mark> in, the <mark class="kv">fetal stage</mark> has begun
The <mark class="kv">fetus</mark> looks unmistakeably human. 

During the <mark class="na">6th month</mark>, organs like the stomach develop enough to give the fetus a good enough chance of surviving if born prematurely
The Fetus is now responsive to sound

Immediately after birth, newborns prefer <mark class="ii">their mother's voice</mark> to another woman or their father
They also prefer their mother's language; if their mother spoke two, they show an interest in both
Just after birth, the melodic ups and downs of newborn cries have a tuneful signature of their mother's native tongue
- Ex, Babies born to french-speaking mothers cried with the rising intonation of French

<mark class="na">7 months in</mark>, fetuses demonstrate learning in other ways
- adapting to a vibrating honking device placed on their mother's abdomen
- 4 weeks later, they recall the sound, evidenced by a mild response compared to control fetuses

## Teratogens
The Placenta usually screens out harmful substances. 
However, some may slip by. 
<mark class="kv">Teratogens</mark> can damage an embryo or fetus
>[!psych] Teratogens
>agens, such as chemicals and viruses, that can reach the embryo or fetus during prenatal development and cause harm

When <mark class="gc">[[alcohol]]</mark> enters a pregnant woman's bloodstream and that of her fetus it <mark class="vi">reduces activity</mark> in both of their <mark class="gc">[[Central Nervous System]]s</mark> 
Alcohol use during pregnancy may prime offspring to like alcohol, and puts them at risk of heavy drinking and <mark class="gc">[[alcohol use disorder]]</mark> during their teen years

Worldwide <mark class="na">$1 \over 10$</mark> women report consuming alcohol while pregnant
Even light drinking, occasional binge drinking, or marijuana smoking can affect the fetal brain

<mark class="ii">Persistent heavy drinking</mark> puts the fetus at risk for <mark class="kv">Congenital (Present at Birth) disabilities</mark>, future behavior problems and lower intelligence
This causes <mark class="kv">fetal alcohol spectrum disorder</mark>
Its most serious form is <mark class="kv">fetal alcohol syndrome</mark>

>[!psych] Fetal Alcohol Syndrome (FAS)
>physical and cognitive function deficits in children caused by their birth mother's heavy drinking during pregnancy. In severe cases, symptoms include a small, out-of-proportion head and distinct facial features

This damage may occur because Alcohol has an <mark class="gc">[[epigenetics|epigenitic]]</mark> effect, leaving <mark class="vi">chemical marks on DNA</mark> that switch genes abnormally on or off
Smoking cigarettes or marijuana during pregnancy also leaves epigenetic scars that may increase vulnerability to stress or addiction

If a pregnant woman experiences <mark class="ii">extreme stress</mark>, the stress hormones flooding her body may indicate a survival threat to the fetus and <mark class="vi">produce an earlier delivery</mark>
Malnourishment, maternal illness, and genetic mutations can put a child at risk for health problems and psychiatric disorders
