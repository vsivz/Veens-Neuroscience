**What is the biological component of gender development?**
Biology <mark class="vi">does not dictate gender</mark>, but it does influence sexual development in two ways. 

- <mark class="ls">Genetically</mark> - We have differing <mark class="vi">sex chromosomes</mark>
- <mark class="ls">Physiologically</mark> - We have differing <mark class="vi">concentrations</mark> of sex hormones whcih trigger other <mark class="ii">anatomical differences</mark>
