
In <mark class="lc">[[Adolescence]]</mark>, a flood of hormones triggers another period of dramatic physical change, when we enter <mark class="kv">puberty</mark>
In this <mark class="na">2-year</mark> period of <mark class="vi">rapid sexual maturation</mark> pronounced <mark class="lc">[[Male-Female Differences]]</mark> emerge
Begins at about <mark class="na">age 10</mark> in girls and <mark class="na">age 12</mark> in boys, though the subtle beginnings appear earlier
A <mark class="na">year or two</mark> before visible physical changes, the <mark class="ii">first stirrings of sexual attraction</mark> are felt

Girls earlier entry into puberty can <mark class="ii">intially</mark> propel them to <mark class="vi">greater height</mark> than boys the same age, but <mark class="vi">boys catch up</mark> when they enter puberty, and by <mark class="na">age 14</mark>, they are usually taller than girls

During these growth spurts <mark class="kv">Primary Sex Characteristics</mark> develop dramatically
So do the <mark class="vi">nonreproductive</mark> <mark class="kv">Secondary Sex Characteristics</mark> 

>[!psych] Primary Sex Characteristics
>the body structures (ovaries, testes, and external genitalia) that make sexual reproducton possible

>[!psych] Secondary Sex Characteristics
>nonreproductive sexual traits, such as female breasts and hips, male voice quality, and body hair

For boys, puberty's landmark is the <mark class="kv">spermarche</mark>, which often occurs first during sleep, and usually happens by about <mark class="na">age 14</mark>
For girls, puberty's landmark is the <mark class="kv">menarche</mark>, which usually happents within an year of age <mark class="na">$2 {1 \over 2}$</mark> 
<mark class="gt">[[Nature vs. Nurture]]</mark>: scientists have identified nearly <mark class="na">250</mark> genes that predict age at menarche. But environment matters too. Early menarche is more likely following stresses related to poverty, father absence, sexual abuse, <mark class="lc">[[insecure attachments]]</mark>, or a history of the mother <mark class="lc">[[Prenatal Development#Teratogens|smoking during pregnancy]]</mark>. 
Girls in various countries are reaching puberty <mark class="ii">earlier today than in the past</mark>. Suspected triggers include increased body fat, increased hormone-mimicking chemicals in the diet & increased stresses related to family disruption.
