
Research shows that:

<mark class="ls">National Wealth</mark> is significant. 
People in countries where most are secure tend to be happier

<mark class="ls">Personal Income</mark> predicts happiness, up to a <mark class="vi">satiation point</mark>
Having enough money to eat and feel control predicts greater happiness.
But this power is strongest at lower incomes, and levels off after a point

<mark class="ls">rising economic tides</mark> do not produce increased happiness
Happiness has gone down slightly despite large increases in buying power in the last 50 years. 

<mark class="ls">extreme inequality is socially toxic</mark> 
In countries and states with greater inequality, those with lower incomes have worse physical and mental health, and more social problems
