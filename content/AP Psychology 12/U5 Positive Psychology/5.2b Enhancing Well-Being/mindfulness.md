Numerous studies have confirmed that [[meditation]] is very beneficial

![[mindfulness meditation|clean no-t]]

Some researchers caution that mindfulness may be overhyped, and may in fact increase anxiety

Studies indicate that

mindfulness <mark class="ls">strengthens connections</mark>among brain regions
Affected regions include those associated with:
- focusing attention
- processing what we see and hear
- being reflective and aware

Mindfulness activates brain regions associated with more <mark class="ls">reflective awareness</mark> 
When labeling emotions, mindful people show less activation in the [[amygdala]], and more activation in the [[Prefrontal cortex]], which aids emotional regulation

Mindfulness <mark class="ls">calms brain activation</mark> in emotional situations
Emotionally unpleasant images trigger weaker electrical brain responses in mindful people
