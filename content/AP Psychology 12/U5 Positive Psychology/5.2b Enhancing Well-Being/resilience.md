>[!psych] resilience
> the personal strength that helps people cope with stress and recover from adversity and even trauma

***
^nonAP

### Key Components

**Cognitive Flexibility & Optimism**
- Reframing challenges in a positive or constructive way
- Engaging in [[cognitive reappraisal]], changing their perspective on [[stress|stressful]] situations
**Emotional Regulation**
- Good management of stress, fear & frustration
- Adaptive Coping Strategies (eg. mindfulness, problem-solving), rather than avoidance or denial
**Interal LOC**
- An [[internal locus of control]]: belief that one has control over their own fate
**Social Support**
- Strong relationships with friends, family & community
- [[Temperament and Parenting|attachment theory]] suggests that [[Secure Attachment|secure]] relationships in early life foster resilience in adulthood
**Sense of Purpose**
- Having goals, values or sense of meaning in life
**Neurobiological Factors**
- The [[HPA axis]] regulates stress response; resilient individuals often have adaptive stress [[hormones|hormone]] regulation
- Strong connections between the [[Prefrontal cortex]] and the [[amygdala]] play crucial roles in resilience
