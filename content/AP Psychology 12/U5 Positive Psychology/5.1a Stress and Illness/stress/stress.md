We may experience stress as 
- positive and motivating: <mark class="ls">eustress</mark>
- negative and debilitating: <mark class="ls">distress</mark>


>[!psych] stress
>the process by which we perceive and respond to certain events, called stressors, that we appraise as threatening or challenging


Someone's physical and emotional responses to <mark class="kv">stressors</mark> are called <mark class="kv">stress responses</mark>

- First we assess- appraise- an event as a stressor: <mark class="ls">primary appraisal</mark>
- Then we assess our ability to respond to it: <mark class="ls">secondary appraisal</mark>

Stressors can have positive effects
- momentary stress can mobilize the [[immune system]]
- In games and athletic contests, uncertainty of who will win makes it enjoyable
- Experiencing some stress builds resilience

But Extreme or prolonged stress can harm us
[[ACEs]] can influence long-term stress responses

Stressors fall into three main types:
- catastrophes
- significant life changes
- daily hassles
