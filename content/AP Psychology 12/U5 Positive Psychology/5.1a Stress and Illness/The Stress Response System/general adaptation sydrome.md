>[!psych] general adaptation syndrome (GAS)
> Selye's concept of the body's adaptive response to stress in three phases - alarm, resistance, exhaustion

If you suffer a physical or emotional trauma
- <mark class="ls">Phase 1</mark>: You have an <mark class="kv">alarm reaction</mark>, as your [[sympathetic nervous system]] is activated. Heart rate zooms, blood gets diverted to skeletal muscles. 
- <mark class="ls">Phase 2:</mark> <mark class="kv">resistance</mark>. your temperature, blood pressure, and respiration remain high. Your [[endocrine system]] pumps [[epinephrine]] and [[norepinephrine]] into your bloodstream
- <mark class="ls">Phase 3:</mark> <mark class="kv">Exhaustion</mark> you become more vulnerable to illness or even collapse or death
