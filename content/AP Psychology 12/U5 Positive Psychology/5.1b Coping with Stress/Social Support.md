Social support - feeling liked and encouraged by intimate friends and family - promotes both happiness and health

Close relationships have predicted happiness and health in both [[individualist]] and [[collectivist]] cultures

People supported by close relationships tend to live longer

Good marriages are a key predictor to good health, but the quality of the marriage matters more than whether one is married or not

In general, Social Support:
- <mark class="ls">calms</mark> us
- improves our<mark class="ls">sleep</mark>
- reduces blood pressure
- fosters a stronger [[immune system]]

Close relationships give an opportunity for a form of open therapy, a chance to confide painful feelings, which is important at reducing [[stress]]
