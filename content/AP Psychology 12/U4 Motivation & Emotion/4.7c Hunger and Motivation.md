Hunger pangs correspond to our stomach contractions, but it also has other causes

>[!psych] glucose
>the form of sugar that circulates in the blood ant provides the major source of energy for body tissues. When its level is low, we feel hunger


Brain areas in the [[hippocampus]], [[hypothalamus]], and other areas monitor blood chemistry and incoming information about body's state

Appetite [[hormones]]:
- Ghrelin: secreted by an empty stomach: signaling hunger
- Leptin: secreted by fat cells: when abundant, causes the brain to increase metabolism and decrease hunger
- Orexin: secreted by the hypothalamus: triggers hunger
- PYY: secreted by the digestive tract: sends 'I'm not hungry' to the brain

>[!psych] basal metabolic rate
>the body's resting rate of energy expenditure


The body may have a set point - biologically fixed tendency to maintain optimal weight, or a looser settling point

**What is the role of culture?**

Hunger reflects memory of 
- when we last ate
- when we should eat again

Humans in general prefer certain tastes
individual prefereneces are also influenced by culture conditioning and situation

some taste preferences have survival value

situational influences include:
- presence of others
- serving size 
- variety of foods offered
