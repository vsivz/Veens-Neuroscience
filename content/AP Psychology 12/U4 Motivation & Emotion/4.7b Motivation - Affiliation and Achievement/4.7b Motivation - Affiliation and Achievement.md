Social bonding boosted our ancestors early chances of survival
- adults who formed attachments were more likely to survive, reproduce, and co-nurture offspring to maturity
- attachment bonds encouraged caregivers to protect kids
- cooperating with others enhanced hunting and protection

Having a <mark class="kv">social identity</mark> - feeling part of a group - boosts people's heealth and well-being

**Why do we achive Goals?**

>[!psych] achievement motivation
>the desire for significant accomplishment, mastery of skills or ideas, control, and for attaining a high standard

Higher achievement motivation $\rightarrow$ greater success

excessive <mark class="vi">extrinsic</mark> rewards can undermine <mark class="vi">intrinsic</mark> motivation

to achieve our own goals:
- make a resolution
- announce the goal
- develop an implementation plan
- create short term rewards
- monitor and record progress
- create a supportive environment
- transform behavior $\rightarrow$ habit
