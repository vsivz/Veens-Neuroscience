According to <mark class="kv">incentive theory</mark>, we are not only *pushed* by a need to reduce drives, but we are also *pulled* by <mark class="kv">incentives</mark>.

![[incentive|clean no-t]]

If positive, these will increase our <mark class="gc">[[dopamine]]</mark> levels
This causes our underlying drives (ex. food, love) to become active impulses

The more these impulses are satisfied and reinforces, the <mark class="vi">stronger the drive will become</mark>

When there is <mark class="vi">both</mark> a need and an incentive, we feel strongly driven.
