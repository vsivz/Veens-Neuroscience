Our <mark class="kv">motivations</mark> arse from the interplay between <mark class="kt">[[Nature vs. Nurture|Nature and Nurture]]</mark>:
The bodily push and the pulls from our personal experiences, thoughs and culture

![[motivation|clean no-t]]

Psychologists have viewed motivated behavior from <mark class="na">four perspectives</mark>:
- <mark class="ls">Instinct Theory</mark>, now replaced by the evolutionary perspective focuses on genetically predisposed behaviors. 
- <mark class="ls">Drive-reduction theory</mark> focuses on how we respond to inner pushes and external pulls
- <mark class="ls">Arousal Theory</mark> focuses on finding the right level of stimulation
- <mark class="ls">Abraham Maslow's hierarchy of needs</mark> focuses on the priority of some needs over others
