
>[!psych] instinct
>a complex behavior that is rigidly patterened throughout a species and is unlearned

Such unlearned behaviors include <mark class="gc">[[imprinting]]</mark>
A few human behaviors, such as <mark class="gc">[[Infant Reflexes]]</mark> exhibit unlearned fixed patterns

But many more are directed both by physiological needs and psychological wants.

Instincts cannot explain most human motives, but the underlying assumption lies in <mark class="gc">[[evolutionary psychology]]</mark>

<mark class="gt">[[genes]]</mark> do predispose some behavior
