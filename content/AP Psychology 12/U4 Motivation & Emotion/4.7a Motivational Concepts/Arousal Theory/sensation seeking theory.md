- <mark class="ls">experience seeking</mark> -a desire for novel sensory or mental experiences
- <mark class="ls">thrill or adventure seeking</mark> - an attractinon to risky or fear-inspring activities
- <mark class="ls">disinhibition</mark> - a loss of self control
