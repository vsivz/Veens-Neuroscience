>[!psych] bipolar disorders
>a group of [[psychological disorder|disorders]] in which a person alternates between the hopelessness and lethargy of [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]] and the overexcited state of [[mania]]


Bipolar disorders can take different forms depending on the severity of symptoms and how long they last.

###### Biological Basis of Bipolar
Bipolar is among one of the most [[heritability|heritable]] psychiatric conditions.
If one identical twin has a bipolar disorder, there is a $\frac{7}{10}$ chance of a co-twin developing it, even for twins raised apart. 

Studies have shown many [[1.4b Brain Structure|brain]] differences in Bipolar.
During depression, brain activity slows, during mania, it increases. t
Depression often lowers activity in the brain's [[reward centers]], whereas manic states increase it. 

[[norepinephrine]], which increase arousal and boosts mood, is scarce during depression and overabundant during mania. Drugs that decrease mania reduce norepinephrine levels.

[[serotonin]] is also scarce or inactive during depression, but its level increases when depression fades.
