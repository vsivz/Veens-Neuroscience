>[!psych] bipolar II disorder
>a less severe form of [[5.4b Bipolar Disorders|Bipolar]] [[psychological disorder|disorder]], in which people move between [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]] and a milder hypomania

>[!dsm] Info from the DSM-5-TR
>diagnosing bipolar II requires one hypomanic episode *and* one major depressive episode
>
>a hypomanic episode shares the same diagnostic symptoms as a manic episode
>The difference is that a hypomanic episode must last more than 4, but less than 7 days, and require no hospitalization or psychosis
>In addition, mania is much more likely to cause functional impairment
