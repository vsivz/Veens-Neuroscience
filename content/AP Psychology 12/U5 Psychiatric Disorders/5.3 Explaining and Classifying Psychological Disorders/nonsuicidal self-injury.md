Some people, mostly [[Adolescence|adolescents]] and females engage in nonsuicidal self-injury (NSSI)
Ex. they may, cut or burn their skin, hit themselves, or insert objects under their nails or skin

Those who engage in NSSI often have experienced bullying, harassment, or stress
They are generally less able to tolerate and regulate emotional distress. And they are often both self-critical and impulsive

NSSI is often self-reinforcing

People who engage in NSSI may:
- find relief from intense negative thoughts through distraction of pain
- attract attention and possibly get help
- relieve guilt by punishing themselves
- get others to change their negative behavior (bullying, criticism)
- fit in with a peer group

NSSI typically does not lead to [[suicide]], but it is a risk factor for suicidal thoughts and attempts, especially for those with a [[5.4b Bipolar Disorders|bipolar disorder]]
