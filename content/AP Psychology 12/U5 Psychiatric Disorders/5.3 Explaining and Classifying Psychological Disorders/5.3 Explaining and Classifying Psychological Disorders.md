In the middle ages, people believed that strange behaviors were the work of strange forces, 
- movement of stars
- godlike powers
- evil spirits

To drive out the demons, people suffering from "madness", have subjected to 
- being chained
- being caged
- genital mutilation
- beatings
- removal of teeth or lengths of intestines
- transfusions of animal blood

Reformers such as Philippe Pinel opposed these methods
He realized that demons did not cause this "madness"
He believed that madness was instead a sickness of the mind, caused by severe stress and inhumane conditions
He argued that curing the illness required *moral treatment*, like uplifting patients by talking to them
In some places these feudal practices still remain
