About <mark class="na">half</mark> of people with a disorder experience first symptoms by their <mark class="na">mid-teens</mark>, and <mark class="na">$3\over 4$</mark> by their <mark class="na">mid-twenties</mark>

Among the earliest to appear are symptoms of:
- [[antisocial personality disorder]], at <mark class="na">median age 8</mark>
- [[phobia|phobias]], at <mark class="na">mediann age 10</mark>

Later at around <mark class="na">median age 20</mark> symptoms appear for:
- [[alcohol use disorder]]
- [[obsessive-compulsive disorder]]
- [[5.4b Bipolar Disorders|bipolar disorder]]
- [[schizophrenia]]

[[major depressive disorder]] often appears later, at <mark class="na">median age 25</mark>
