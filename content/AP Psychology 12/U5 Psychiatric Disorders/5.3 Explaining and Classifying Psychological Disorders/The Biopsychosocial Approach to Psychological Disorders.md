Two disorders - Major Depressive Disorder and Schizophrenia - occur worldwide

Other disorders tend to be <mark class="vi">culture-bound</mark>

In Latin America, some experience *susto* - severe [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]] or panic in response to an emotional trauma or fear of black magic

The eating disorders [[anorexia nervosa]] and [[bulimia nervosa]] occur mostly in food-abundant Western culture

Such disorders may share an underlying dynamic (ex. anxiety) but while differing in symptoms manifested in a particular culture

[[Interaction Models]] can help us understand the interacting influences on psychological disorders
The [[Biopsychosocial Approach]] emphasizes an inseparability of mind and body

It gave rise to the diathesis-stress model (also called the vulnerability-stress model)

Research on epigenetics support the diathesis-stress model, by showing how our DNA and environment interact
