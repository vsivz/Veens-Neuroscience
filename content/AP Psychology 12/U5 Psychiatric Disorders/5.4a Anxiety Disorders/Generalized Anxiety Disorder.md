
>[!psych] generalized anxiety disorder
>an [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]] [[psychological disorder|disorder]] in which a person is continually tense, apprehensive and in a state of [[Autonomic Nervous System]] arousal


GAD is marked by excessive or uncontrollable worry that persists for 6 months or more

$\frac{2}{3}$ of those with this condition are [[Male-Female Differences|women]], worry continually, and they are often jittery, on edge and sleep-deprived

Their ANS arousal may show through furrowed brows, twitching eyelids, trembling, perspiration and fidgeting

Concentration may suffer

Typically, those with GAD cannot identify, relieve, or avoid their anxiety
The anxiety is free-floating

GAD and [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]] often go hand in hand, but even without depression, GAD tends to be disabling
