
Some people experience intense anxiety that escalates into a panic attack, a minutes-long episode of intense fear that something horrible is about to happen. 

3% of people have a panic disorder, where panic attacks are recurrent. 
Worries about anxiety, including fear of another panic attack, can amplify anxiety symptoms and trigger another panic attack. 
After several panic attacks, people may come to fear the fear itself.
This can trigger [[agoraphobia]].
