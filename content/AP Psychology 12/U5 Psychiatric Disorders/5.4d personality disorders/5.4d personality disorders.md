>[!psych] personality disorders
>a group of [[psychological disorder|disorders]] characterized by enduring inner experiences or behavior patterns that differ from someone's cultural [[norms]] and expectations, are pervasive and inflexible, begin in [[Adolescence]] or early adulthood, are stable over time, and cause distress or impairment


There are 10 of these disorders, and are organized into 3 clusters.
