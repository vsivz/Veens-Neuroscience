In this cluster of [[5.4d personality disorders|personality disorders]], people appear *dramatic, emotional, or erratic*

**Antisocial Personality Disorder**
This Cluster B personality disorder is characterized by a pervasive pattern of *disregard for*, and *violation of*, the *rights of others*, often involving deceitful, manipulative, and unlawful behaviors, and a *lack of remorse* for these actions.
