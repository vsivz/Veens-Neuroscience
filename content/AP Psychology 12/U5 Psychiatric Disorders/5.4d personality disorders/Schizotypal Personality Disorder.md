Schizotypal Personality [[psychological disorder|disorder]] is unique that it is both a [[Cluster A Personality Disorders|cluster A personality disorder]] and a [[5.4c Schizophrenia Spectrum Disorders|Schizophrenia Spectrum Disorder]] 
It is characterized by:
- *Intense Social Discomfort*, and difficulty forming close relationships, due to *paranoia*
- *Behavioral Eccentricity*, defying social [[norms]]
- *Distorted Cognitions or Perceptions*, ex. believing that unrelated events are very significant, odd beliefs, though not full hallucinations
