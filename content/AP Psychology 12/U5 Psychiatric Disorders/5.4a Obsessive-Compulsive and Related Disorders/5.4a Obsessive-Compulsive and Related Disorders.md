Obsessive-compulsive and related [[psychological disorder|disorders]] are characterized by obsessions and compulsions. 

*Obsessive thoughts* are unwanted and seemingly unending
*Compulsive behaviors* are responses to those thoughts

The most prominent is [[obsessive-compulsive disorder]]

![[obsessive-compulsive disorder|clean no-t]]

Many people have minimal symptoms that resemble this. 
They cross the line between normality and disorder only when they *persistently interfere* with everyday living
About <mark class="na">2%</mark> of people, often during their late teens or early adulthood, cross that line.

According to a study of 144 Swedes, with age, many people report lessening symptoms, although only <mark class="na">1 in 5</mark> completely recover

Some people also experience other OCD-related disorders

![[hoarding disorder|clean no-t]]
![[body dysmorphic disorder|clean no-t]]
![[trichotillomania|clean no-t]]

Brain scans reveal elevated activity in specific [[1.4b Brain Structure|brain]] areas during compulsive behaviors
The [[Angular Cingulate Cortex]], a brain region that monitors our actions and checks for errors, is often especially hyperactive.
