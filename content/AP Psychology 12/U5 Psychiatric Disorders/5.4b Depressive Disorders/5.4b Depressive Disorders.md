>[!psych] depressive disorders
>a group of [[psychological disorder|disorders]] characterized by an enduring sad, empty, or irritable mood, along with physical and cognitive changes that affect a person's ability to function


![[major depressive disorder|clean no-t]]

![[persistent depressive disorder|clean no-t]]

Depression can look different across people
One can be depressed in different ways and for different amounts of time

The WHO declared depression the leading cause of disability worldwide

US depression levels rose dramatically during the COVID pandemic.
Younger adults, [[Male-Female Differences|women]], people of color, and unemployed were hardest hit
At least 1 in 5 health care professionals reported symptoms of depression

![[Seasonal Affective Disorder|clean no-t]]

***
**Behaviors and thoughts change with depression**
Depression may be triggered by a sad or stressful event, but people with depression may feel very sad even when nothing bad happens.
They more often recall and expect negative events

**Depression is widespread, and women are at greatest risk**
Globally, women's risk for major depressive disorder is roughly double mens
This fits a general pattern, where women are more vulnerable to disorders involving internal states, like [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]]
Women also experience more situations that increase their risk for depression, such as receiving less pay for equal work, juggling multiple roles, and caring for children and elderly family members
Men's disorders tend to be more external, alcohol use disorder, disorders related to antisocial conduct and impulse control.

**Most major depressive episodes end on their own**
Therapy often speeds recovery
Even without professional help, most people recover from depression

**Depression sometimes returns**
For many people, depression comes, and eventually, it goes
For 20% of people, the condition will be chronic
An enduring recovery is more likely if:
- the first episode strikes later in life
- there were no previous episodes
- the person experiences minimal physical or psychological stress
- there is ample social support

**Stress and negative events often precede depression**
About 1 in 4 diagnoses are brought about by a significant loss or trauma, such as:
- loved one's death
- ruptured marriage
- physical assault
- lost job
moving to a new culture also increases risk for depression, especially for younger people who have not yet [[identity fomation|formed their identity]]

**Depression is increasing**
Compared with previous generations:
- depression strikes earlier
- depression strikes more often
The highest rates are among older teens and young adults
From 2009-2020, depression doubled among US 12-to 17-year-olds
When depression strikes early, male-female differences are increased further

***
There is a genetic component to depression
If one identical twin is diagnosed with major depressive disorder, there is a 50% chance the other twin will too
Research teams have estimated the [[heritability]] of MDD at 40%
But Nurture also matters. A study showed that adopted children who had a biological parent with depression had a significantly reduced chance of MDD than a child who remained with a biological parent with depression.

Depression is associated with low levels of [[norepinephrine]] and [[serotonin]].
This is why some [[antidepressants]] increase these neurotransmitters, either by blocking [[reuptake]], like [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitor|SSRIs]] and [[Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitor|SNRIs]], or their chemical breakdown, like [[Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors|MAOIs]].

Repetitive physical exercise, such as jogging, reduces depression in part because it increases Serotonin. 

Excessive [[alcohol]] use also correlates with depression, partly because depression can increase alcohol use, but mostly because alcohol misuse leads to depression. 

***
Many depressed people have very negative views of themselves, their situation and their future

Expecting the worst, depressed people magnify bad experiences and minimize good ones
Their self-defeating beliefs and negative [[explanatory style]] feed their depression

These beliefs may arise from [[learned helplessness]]

![[Drawing 2024-12-31 00.24.04.excalidraw|1000]]
Stressful experiences, interpreted through a brooding, negative explanatory style creates a hopeless depressed state that impacts how a person thinks. This fuel further stressful experiences.
