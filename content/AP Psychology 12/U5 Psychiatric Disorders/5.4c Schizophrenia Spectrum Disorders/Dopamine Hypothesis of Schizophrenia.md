
The dopamine hypothesis is one possible answer to the cause of [[5.4c Schizophrenia Spectrum Disorders|schizophrenia]]

When researchers examined schizophrenia patients' brains after death, they found an excess number of [[dopamine receptors]]

The resulting hyper-responsive [[dopamine]] system may intensify brain signals in schizophrenia, creating positive symptoms such as hallucinations and paranoia. Drugs that block dopamine receptors often lessen these symptoms, and drugs that increase dopamine levels sometimes intensify them.
