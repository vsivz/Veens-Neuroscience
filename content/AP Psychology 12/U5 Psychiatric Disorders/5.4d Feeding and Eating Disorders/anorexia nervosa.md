>[!psych] anorexia nervosa
>an [[5.4d Feeding and Eating Disorders|eating disorder]] in which a person (usually an [[Adolescence|adolescent]] [[Male-Female Differences|female]]) maintains a starvation diet despite being significantly underweight, and has an inaccurate self-perception; sometimes accompanied by excessive exercise


Anorexia often begins as an attempt to lose weight, but the dieting becomes habitual.
Regardless of their actual weight, the person feels fat, fears being fat, and focuses obsessively on losing weight
