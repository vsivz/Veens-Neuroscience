
*Motor Disorders* impair people's ability to communicate, interact with others, or perform necessary tasks.

These disorders usually appear before age 18, and sometimes as young as age 5

They affect the parts of the [[1.4b Brain Structure|brain]] involved in planning and executing actions, causing people to display movements that are atypical or involuntary.
