Those with *tic disorders* produce *sudden*, *rapid*, and *involuntary* movements or vocalizations

*Tourette's disorder* is a type of motor disorder. People may repetitively say 'No Way' and turn their head to the side, even if they recognize that this is not appropriate behavior. In rare cases, they may curse or make vulgar gestures.
