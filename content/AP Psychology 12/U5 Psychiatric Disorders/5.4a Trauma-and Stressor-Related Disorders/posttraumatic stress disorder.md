>[!psych] posttraumatic stress disorder
>a [[psychological disorder|disorder]] characterized by haunting memories, nightmares, hypervigilance, avoidance of trauma-related stimuli, social withdrawal, jumpy [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]], numbness of feeling, and/or [[insomnia]] that lingers for 4 weeks or more after a traumatic experience
