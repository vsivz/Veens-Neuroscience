>[!psych] trauma-and stressor-related disorders
>a group of [[psychological disorder|disorders]] in which exposure to a traumatic or stressful event is followed by psychological distress

In one study of 104,000 veterans returning from Iraq and Afghanistan, <mark class="na">25%</mark> were diagnosed with a psychological disorder.
The most frequent diagnosis was [[posttraumatic stress disorder|PTSD]]

![[posttraumatic stress disorder|clean no-t]]

Survivors of terror, torture, sexual assault, earthquakes, and refugee displacement have also exhibited PTSD. 
Many people display *survivor resiliency*. About half of trauma survivors report *posttraumatic growth*

The higher the level of distress, the greater the risk for posttraumatic symptoms. 

***
A history of wartime trauma or child abuse can leave long-term [[epigenetics|epigenetic marks]]
these can increase the chance that genetic vulnerability to a disorder like PTSD will be expressed
