I have significantly enlarged the [[Prefrontal cortex]] to reflect Stalin's strong ability to plan. The most well known example of this was his Five Year Plans. These were very well thought out plans, where he directed the Soviet economy from an industrial backwater far behind the rest of Europe, to competing directly with the United States. Furthermore, his planning of coordinating the Red Army turned the tide of the second world war, ultimately freeing Europe from the Nazis. Another lesser known example occurred in his younger years, during the Tsarist Regime. In order to fundraise for the Bolsheviks, Stalin carefully planned out a bank robbery which successfully raised the money they needed.
