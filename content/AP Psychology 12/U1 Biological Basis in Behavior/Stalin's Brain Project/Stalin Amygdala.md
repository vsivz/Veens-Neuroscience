The [[amygdala]] has been shrunk to reflect Stalin's lessened emotions, particularily fear. Before involvement in high-level Bolshevik politics, Stalin would take place in grassroots labor efforts. Under immense threat from the Tsarist regime, he worked fearlessly to organize strikes to improve working conditons. This was most notable in Baku in 1908, where a large strike was met with a violent, repressive crackdown, yet Stalin continued mobilizing workers.
	In addition, Stalin played a key role in masterminding a 1907 bank robbery to raise money for the communists. In doing so, he risked the wrath of the Tsarist Regime. Furthermore, the Tsarist regime exiled him to Siberia because of his revolutionary activities. He would once again demonstrate a lack of fear, escaping and making the arduous trek across long snowy distances. Later, after being arrested and exiled again, he once again made the daring escape back home.
	Stalin also didn't show emotion in general, with many often describing him as cold and emotionless. For example, when Nazis captured his son, and demanded an unfair prisoner swap, Stalin did not think emotionally at all, and refused the trade.
