just below (hypo) the [[Thalamus]]

The Hypothalamus is an important link in the command chain governing body maintenance

Various neural clusters in the Hypothalamus influence:
- hunger
- thirst
- body temperature
- sexual behavior

Together, they help to maintain a [[homeostasis|homeostatic]] internal state

It helps govern the [[endocrine system]]
It is linked to emotion and reward

To monitor your body state, the hypothalamus tunes into your blood chemistry and any incoming orders from other brain parts.
