

The Amygdala enables <mark class="vi">aggression and fear</mark>

Two lima-bean sized neural clusters in the [[Limbic System]]

People with Amygdala lesions often display reduced arousal to fear and anger arousing stimuli. One woman displayed no fear to being threatened with a gun.

However
The Amygdala is engaged with other mental phenomena as well
And fear and aggression correlates with neural activity in many areas in the brain, not just the Amygdala
