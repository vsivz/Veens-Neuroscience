The Thalamus sits atop the [[brainstem]], it's part of the [[forebrain]]

This pair of egg-shaped structures acts as the brain's <mark class="vi">sensory control center</mark>
It receives information from all senses, <mark class="ii">except smell</mark>, and routes that info to the relevant brain regions
It also receives some replies from those regions, which it routes to the [[medulla oblongata|medulla]] and [[Cerebellum]]
