
**Physical Characteristics**:
- part of the [[hindbrain]]
- extends from the rear of the [[brainstem]]
- baseball-sized
- its name means 'little brain', which its two wrinkled halves resemble

The Cerebellum, along with the [[Basal Ganglia]], enables nonverbal and [[procedural]] learning.

With assistance from the [[Pons]], it coordinates voluntary movement

It contains <mark class="na">more than half</mark> the brain's [[neurons]]
