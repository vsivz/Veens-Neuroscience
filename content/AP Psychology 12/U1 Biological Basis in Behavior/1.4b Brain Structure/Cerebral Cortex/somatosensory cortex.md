>[!psych] somatosensory cortex
>a [[Cerebral Cortex]] area at the front of the [[parietal lobes]], parallel to and just behind the [[motor cortex]], that receives info from the skin senses, such as touch and temperature, and from the movement of body parts. 
>The more sensitive the body region, the larger the somatosensory cortex area devoted to it.
