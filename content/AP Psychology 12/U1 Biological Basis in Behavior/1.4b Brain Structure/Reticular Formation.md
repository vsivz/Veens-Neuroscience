The Reticular Formation is a [[nerves|nerve]] network governed by the reticular activating system

It extends from the spinal cord right up through the [[Thalamus]]; as the spinal cord's sensory input flows up to the Thalamus, some travels through the RF, which <mark class="vi">filters incoming stimuli</mark>and relays whats important to other brain areas

The RF also controls arousal
