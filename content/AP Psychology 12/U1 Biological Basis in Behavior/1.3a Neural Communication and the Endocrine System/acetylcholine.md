ACh is a [[neurotransmitter]] that plays a role in learning and memory
It also enables muscle action, by acting as the messenger at every junction between [[motor neurons]] and skeletal [[muscles]]
When ACh is released to muscle cell receptors, the targeted muscle contracts. 

If ACh transmission is blocked, as happens during some kinds of anesthesia, with some poisons, and with the neuromuscular disease myasthenia gravis, the muscles cannot contract. With [[Alzheimer's disease]], ACh producing neurons deteriorate. 
The result is weakness, difficulties with muscle control, or paralysis
