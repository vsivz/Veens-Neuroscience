Short for endogenous (produced within) [[morphine]], are our own naturally occurring [[opioids]]
These endorphins explain good feelings such as
- "runners high"
- painkilling effects of acupunture
- indifference to pain in some severely injured people

Physician Lewis Thomas called the endorphins "a biologically universal act of mercy"

When flooded with opioid drugs, the brain - to maintain its chemical balance - may stop producing its own natural opioids
When the drug is withdrawn, the brain may then be deprived of any form of opioid, causing intense discomfort
