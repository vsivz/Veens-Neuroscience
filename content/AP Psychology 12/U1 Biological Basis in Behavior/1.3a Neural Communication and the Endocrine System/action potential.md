>[!psych] action potential
> a neural impulse; a brief electrical charge that travels down an [[axon]]


Depending on the type of fiber, a neural impulse travels at speeds ranging from 3 kilometers per hour, to more than 320 kilometers per hour
But even its top speed is 3 million times slower than that of [[Science/Physics11/Electric Circuits/Electric Circuits|electricity]]; we measure brain activity in milliseconds and [[computer]] activity in nanoseconds

Like batteries, [[neurons]] generate [[Science/Physics11/Electric Circuits/Electric Circuits|electricity]] from [[Chemistry|chemical events]].
