  >[!psych] synapse
>the junction between the [[axon]] tip of the sending [[neurons|neuron]] and the [[dendrites|dendrite]] or [[cell body]] of the receiving neuron. The tiny gap at this junction is called the *synaptic cleft* or synaptic gap.


When an [[action potential]] reaches the button-like terminals at an axon's end, it triggers the release of [[neurotransmitter]]

![[neurotransmitter|clean no-t]]

Within $1\text{/}10,000$ of a second, the neurotransmitter molecules cross the synaptic gap and bind to the [[Receptor Biology|receptor]] sites as precisely as a key fits a lock. 

(In the case of [[Ionotropic Receptors|ligand-gated ion channels]]) the neurotransmitter unlocks tiny channels at the receiving site, and electrically charged atoms flow in, exciting or inhibiting the receiving neuron's readiness to fire. 
The excess neurotransmitters may then be broken down by enzymes or [[reuptake|reuptaken]]
