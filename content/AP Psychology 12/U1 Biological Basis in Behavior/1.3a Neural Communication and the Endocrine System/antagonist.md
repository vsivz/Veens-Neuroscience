>[!psych] antagonist
>a molecule that inhibits or blocks a [[neurotransmitter]]'s action

They do this by blocking production or release

Botulin is an antagonist that blocks [[acetylcholine|ACh]] release.
small injections of Botulin, known by brand name Botox, smooth wrinkles by paralyzing underlying facial muscles

These antagonists can be similar enough to a neurotransmitter to occupy its receptor site and block it, but not similar enough to stimulate the receptor
