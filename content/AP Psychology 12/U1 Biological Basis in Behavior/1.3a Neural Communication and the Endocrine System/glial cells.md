>[!psych] glial cells (glia)
> cells in the [[1.2 Overview of the Nervous System|Nervous System]] that support, nourish, and protect [[neurons]]; 
> they provide nutrients and insulating [[myelin sheath|myelin]], guide neural connections, and clean up waste after neurons send messages to one another;
> 
> they may also play a role in learning, thinking and memory: by 'chatting' with neurons, they participate in information transmission and memory
