###### Overview

Ecstasy is the street name for MDMA, or methylenedioxymethamphetamine, known in its powder form as Molly
Its a [[stimulants|stimulant]] and a mild [[hallucinogens|hallucinogen]]

Its an [[Amphetamine|Amphetamine]] derivative, so it releases [[dopamine]], but its major effect is releasing [[serotonin]] and blocking its [[reuptake]]

The onset of action of an ecstasy pill is about 30 minutes
It lasts for about 3 to 4 hours

Its popularity first soared globally in the late 90s as a club drug taken at nightclubs and all-night dance parties

It has a dehydrating effect which, when combined with prolonged dancing, can lead to severe overheating, increased blood pressure and death

Another risk is that repeated leaching of brain serotonin can permanently damage serotonin-producing [[neurons]], increasing risk of permanently depresse d mood

It also suppresses the [[immune system]], impairs memory, slows thought, and disrupts [[sleep]] by interfering with serotonin's control of the circadian clock
