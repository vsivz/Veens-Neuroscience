>[!psych] myelin sheath
>a fatty tissue layer segmentally encasing the [[axon]]s of some [[neurons]]; It enables vastly greater transmission speed as [[action potential|neural impulses]] hop from one node to the next

As myelin is laid down until age 25, neural efficiency, judgement, and self-control grow. 
If the myelin sheath degenerates, this deterioration leads to diminished control and slower reaction time. 
In extreme cases, it leads to [[multiple sclerosis]]
