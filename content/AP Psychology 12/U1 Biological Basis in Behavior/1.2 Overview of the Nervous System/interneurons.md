>[!psych] interneurons
>[[neurons]] within the [[Central Nervous System|brain and spinal cord]]; they communicate internally and process information between the sensory inputs and motor outputs

Our complexity lies mostly in these interneurons. Our [[1.2 Overview of the Nervous System|Nervous System]] has a few million each of sensory and motor neurons, but <mark class="na">billions and billions</mark> of interneurons
