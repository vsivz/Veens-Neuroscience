Marijuana leaves and flowers contain THC - $\Delta9$-tetrahydrocannabinol

It is a mild [[hallucinogens|hallucinogen]]

A joint may induce symptoms of hallucinations, delusions and [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]]

It amplifies sensitivity to colors, sounds, tastes and smells

It relaxes, disinhibits and may produce a euphoric high

People may consume it to counter [[insomnia]]

THC and its by-products linger in the body for more than a week; 

According to the US National Academies of Sciences, Engineering and Medicine, Marijuana use:
- alleviates chronic pain, chemotherapy-related nausea, and muscle soreness among people with multiple sclerosis;
- may offer short-term sleep improvements;
- does not increase risk for tobacco-related diseases such as lung cancer;
- predicts increased risk of traffic accidents, chronic bronchitis, psychosis, social anxiety disorder, and suicidal thoughts; and
- likely contributes to impaired attention, learning, and memory, and possibly to academic underachievement.

The more often someone uses marijuana, especially during [[Adolescence]], the greater the risk of anxiety, [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]], psychosis and [[suicide|suicidal]] behavior
