
>[!psych] substance use disorder
>a disorder characterized by continued [[1.3b Drugs!|substance]] use despite resulting life disruption

A person may be diagnosed with a substance use disorder when drug use continues despite significant life disruptions
The severity of substance use disorder can be
- mild (2-3 of the following indicators)
- moderate (4-5 indicators)
- severe (6+ indicators)

Diminished Control
- Uses more substance, or for longer than intended
- Tries unsuccessfully to regulate use of substance
- Spends much time acquiring, using, or recovering from effects of substance
- Craves the substance

Diminished Social Functioning
- Use disrupts commitments at work, school or home
- Continues use despite social problems
- Causes reduced social, recreational, and work activities

Hazardous Use
- Continues use despite hazards
- Continues use despite worsening physical or psychological problems

Drug Action
- Experiences [[tolerance]]
- Experiences [[withdrawal]]


Addiction gets caused by ever-increasing doses of most psychoactive drugs (including prescription painkillers)
Prompts user to crave drug, continue use despite adverse effects, and struggle when attempting to withdraw from it.

When people have a substance use disorder, they are more likely to *want* the drug than *like* the drug
