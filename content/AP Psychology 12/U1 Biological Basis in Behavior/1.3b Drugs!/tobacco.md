Tobacco products deliver highly addictive [[nicotine]]

Each year tobacco kills nearly 7 million worldwide

Tobacco products include
- cigarettes
- cigars
- chewing tobacco
- pipe tobacco
- snuff
- e-cigarettes

Nicotine is powerfully addictive
attempts to quit tobacco use even within the first weeks often fail
As with other addictions, smokers develop [[tolerance]]
Attempting to quit will bring [[withdrawal]] symptoms, including craving, [[insomnia]], [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]], irritability, and distractibility

Nicotine will tell the [[Central Nervous System]] to release powerful [[neurotransmitter]]s, 
[[epinephrine]] and [[norepinephrine]] will diminish appetite and boost mental efficiency
[[dopamine]] and [[opioids]] will temporarily calm anxiety and reduce pain sensitivty
