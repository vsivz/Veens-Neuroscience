
Alcohol Use Disorder is characterized by prolonged and excessive drinking
It contributes to more than diseases, can shrink the [[1.4b Brain Structure|brain]], and contribute to a premature death

[[Male-Female Differences|Girls and young women]], who have less of a stomach enzyme that digests [[alcohol]], can become addicted to alcohol more quickly than boys and young men do, and they are at risk for [[lungs|lung]], brain, and [[liver]] damage at lower consumption levels

Between 2001-2017, Canadian women's risk for alcohol-related death increased at five times the rate for men
