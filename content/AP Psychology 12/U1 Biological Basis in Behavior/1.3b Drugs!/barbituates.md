
>[!psych] barbituates
>drugs that [[depressants|depress]] [[Central Nervous System]] activity, reducing [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]], but impairing memory and judgment

These are *tranquilizers*
Barbituates, such as Nembutal, Seconal and Amytal are sometimes prescribed to induce sleep or reduce anxiety
In larger doses, they can impair memory and judgment
If combined with [[alcohol]], the total depressive effect on body functions can be lethal
