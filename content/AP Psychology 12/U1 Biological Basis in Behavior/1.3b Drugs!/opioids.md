>[!psych] opioids
>opium and its derivatives; they [[depressants|depress]] neural activity, temporarily lessening pain and [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]]

These include [[heroin]] and its medically prescribed synthetic substitute, methadone
Also include pain-relief *narcotics*, such as codeine, OxyContin, Vicodin and morphine (and [[morphine]]'s dangerous synthetic counterpart [[fentanyl]])

As blissful pleasure replaces pain and anxiety, the user's [[pupil]]s constrict and breathing; lethargy sets in
When repeatedly flooded with a synthetic opioid, the [[1.4b Brain Structure|brain]] eventually stops producing [[endorphins]], its own natural opioids
When the artificial opioid is withdrawn, the brain will lack the normal level of Endorphins, causing extreme discomfort, leading to higher and higher doses, eventually to the point of overdose
