>[!psych] hallucinogens
>psychedelic ("mind-manifesting") [[1.3b Drugs!|drugs]] that distort perceptions and evoke sensory images in the absence of sensory input

Whether provoked to hallucinate by drugs, loss of oxygen, or extreme sensory deprivation, the brain hallucinates in basically the same way
The experience begins with simple geometric forms, such as a spiral
Then come more meaningful images, which may be superimposed on a tunnel
Others may be replays of past emotional experiences

Brains scans of people on a [[LSD]] trip reveal that their [[occipital lobes|visual cortex]] becomes hypersensitive and strongly connected to their brain's emotional centers

As the hallucination peaks, people frequently feel separated from their body and experience dreamlike scenes

Their sense of self dissolves

***

These experiences are very similar to a [[near-death experience]]

![[near-death experience|clean no-t]]

Many describe visions of tunnels, bright lights, a replay of old memories, and out-of-body sensations
These experiences can later enhance spirituality and promote feelings of personal growth
