
Cocaine is a powerfully addictive [[stimulants|stimulant]] from the coca plant

Cocaine is:
- snorted
- injected
- smoked, sometimes as crack cocaine, a faster-working crystallized form

Cocaine blocks the [[reuptake]] of [[dopamine]] primarily, but also [[norepinephrine]] and [[serotonin]], by blocking the [[DAT]], [[NET]] and [[SERT]], creating a euphoric rush

Cocaine increases [[aggression]]

In studies, users who thought they were taking cocaine often had a cocaine-like experience
