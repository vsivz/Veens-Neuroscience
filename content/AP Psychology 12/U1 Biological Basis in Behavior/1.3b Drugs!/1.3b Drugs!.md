###### Methamphetamine
[[Amphetamine|Amphetamine]]s stimulate neural activity, they are the parent drug for the highly addictive [[methamphetamine]], which is chemically similar, but has stronger effects
meth triggers the release of [[dopamine]], leading to 8 or so hours of heightened energy and euphoria

Aftereffects may include:
- irritability
- insomnia
- hypertension
- seizures
- social isolation
- depression
- occasional violent outbursts

Overtime, meth reduces baseline dopamine levels
