Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) is a [[Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy|CBT]] variation that helps to change harmful and even [[suicide|suicidal]] behavior
*Dialectical* means opposing
This [[psychotherapy|therapy]] tries to make peace between two opposing forces - acceptance and change

Therapists create an accepting and encouraging environment.
In individual sessions, clients learn new ways of thinking, helping them *tolerate distress* and *regulate their emotions*

They may also receive training in social skills and in *mindfulness meditation*, which helps alleviate [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]]

Group training sessions offer additional opportunities to practice new skills in a social context, with further practice as homework.
