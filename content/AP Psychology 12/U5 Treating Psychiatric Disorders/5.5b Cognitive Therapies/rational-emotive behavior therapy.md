>[!psych] rational-emotive behavior therapy (REBT)
> a confrontational [[5.5b Cognitive Therapies|cognitive therapy]], developed by Albert Ellis, that vigorously challenges people's illogical, self-defeating attitudes and assumptions

Ellis believed that if you changed people's thinking by revealing the "absurdity" of their self-defeating ideas, you can change self-defeating feelings and enable healthier behaviors.
