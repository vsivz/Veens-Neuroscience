>[!psych] family therapy
>[[psychotherapy|therapy]] that treats people in the context of their family system. Views an individual's unwanted behaviors as influenced by, or directed at, other family members

Family therapists view families as *systems*, where each person's actions trigger reactions from others
For example, a child's rebellion affects, and is affected by, other family tensions
Therapists are often successful in:
- helping family members identify roles within the family's social system
- improve communication
- discover new ways of preventing or resolving conflicts
