>[!psych] virtual reality exposure therapy
>a [[exposure therapies|exposure therapy]] that treats [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]] through creative electronic simulations in which people can safely face specific fears, such as flying, spiders, or public speaking

This often gets used if anxiety-arousing situations, such as flying, heights, particular animals, and public speaking, are too expensive, difficult or embarrassing to re-create
