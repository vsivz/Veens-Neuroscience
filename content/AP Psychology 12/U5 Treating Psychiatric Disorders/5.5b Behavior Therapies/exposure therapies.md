>[!psych] exposure therapies
>behavioral techniques, such as [[systematic desensitization]] and [[virtual reality exposure therapy]], that treat [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxieties]] by exposing people (in imaginary or actual situations) to the things they fear and avoid

These therapies are a good example of [[Applied Behavior Analysis]] - using what we know about conditioning to address disordered behaviors. 

Therapists try to change people's reactions by repeatedly exposing them to [[stimulus|stimuli]] that trigger unwanted reactions. 
Someone will initially be annoyed by loud traffic noise from their new apartment, but with repeated exposure, will adapt. 
Same with people who have fear reactions to specific events, such as those with [[posttraumatic stress disorder|PTSD]].
By being exposed repeatedly to the situation that once petrified them, they can learn to react less anxiously. 

Exposure therapy is neither pleasant nor easy.
