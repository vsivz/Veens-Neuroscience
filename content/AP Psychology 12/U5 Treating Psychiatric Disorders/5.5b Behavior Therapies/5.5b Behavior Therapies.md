>[!psych] Behavior Therapy
>[[psychotherapy|therapy]] that uses [[3.7 Learning|learning]] principles to reduce unwanted behaviors

Behavior therapists use the [[behaviorism|behavioral perspective]] to assume that problem behaviors are the problem. 
Using learning principles, they offer clients strategies for reducing unwanted behaviors.

But there are some critics of behavior modification.
- people may become dependent on [[extrinsic motivation|extrinsic]] rewards. But supporters believe that behaviors will endure if therapists wean people from the tokens by shifting them toward other, real-life rewards like social approval
- is it right for one human to control another's behavior? those who set up token economies deprive people something they desire, and decide what behaviors to reinforce. Advocates argue that control already exists, destructive behavior patterns are being maintained and perpetuated by natural reinforcers. Also an improved life justifies temporary deprivation.
