Although influenced by [[Sigmund Freud|Freud]]'s ideas, Psychodynamic Therapists ignore the [[Freudian Personality Structure]], and use the [[psychodynamic theories]]
They try help people understand their current symptoms by focusing on *important relationships and events*

>[!psych] psychodynamic therapy
>[[psychotherapy|therapy]] deriving from the [[5.5a Psychoanalysis|Psychoanalytic]] tradition; views individuals as responding to [[unconscious]] forces and childhood experiences, and seeks to enhance self-insight

Meetings occur once or twice a week (rather than several times weekly), and often for only a few weeks or months. 

Clients may use [[free association]] or have their dreams interpreted, but will do so face-to-face (in-person or online) than while lying on a couch, out of the FOV of the therapist.
