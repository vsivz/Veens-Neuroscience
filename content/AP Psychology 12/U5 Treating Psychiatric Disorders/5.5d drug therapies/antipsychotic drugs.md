>[!psych] antipsychotic drugs
>[[1.3b Drugs!|drugs]] used to treat [[5.4c Schizophrenia Spectrum Disorders|schizophrenia]] and other forms of severe thought disorders


It was discovered that certain drugs, used for other medical purposes, calmed hallucinations or [[delusions]] 

First generation Antipsychotics, ex. [[chlorpromazine]], dampened responsiveness to irrelevant [[stimulus|stimuli]]
Thus, they most helped patients with *positive* symptoms of schizophrenia, such as auditory hallucinations and paranoia.
They were less effective changing negative symptoms, such as apathy and withdrawal

The molecules of most conventional antipsychotic drugs are similar enough to [[dopamine]] to occupy its [[Receptor Biology|receptor]] sites and *block its activity*
This reinforces the [[Dopamine Hypothesis of Schizophrenia]]

Antipsychotics have powerful side effects:
- sluggishness
- tremors
- twitches similar to Parkinson's disease
