the simple salt lithium is a very effective [[mood stabilizers|mood stabilizer]], used primarily to help with [[5.4b Bipolar Disorders|Bipolar Disorders]]
This helps to prevent or ease [[mania|manic episodes]], and to a lesser extent [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]]

About 7 in 10 people with bipolar disorders benefit from a long-term daily dose of this.

Taking lithium also correlates with a lower [[suicide]] risk among those with bipolar, *one sixth* the risk of those not taking it.

Naturally occurring lithium in drinking water has also been correlated with lower suicide rates and lower crime rates.
