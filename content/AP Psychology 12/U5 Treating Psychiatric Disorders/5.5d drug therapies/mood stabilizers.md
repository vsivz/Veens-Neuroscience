Mood stabilizers, are a class of [[5.5d drug therapies|psychotropic drugs]] that treat mood disorders

Depakote is an example. It was originally used to treat [[epilepsy]], but then was found to be effective in controlling the [[mania|manic episodes]] of [[5.4b Bipolar Disorders|Bipolar Disorders]]
