Long term use of first-generation [[antipsychotic drugs|antipsychotics]] can cause *tardive dyskinesia*
It is a movement [[psychological disorder|disorder]] related to the regulation of [[dopamine]] in the [[1.2 Overview of the Nervous System|Nervous System]], which produces involuntary movements of the facial muscles, tongue and limbs. 
Newer-generation antipsychotics have fewer of these effects, although they may increase risk of obesity and diabetes
