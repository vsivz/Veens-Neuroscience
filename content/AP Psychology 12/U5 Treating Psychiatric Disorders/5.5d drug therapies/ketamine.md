For those at risk of [[suicide]], researchers are also exploring the possibility of quicker acting [[antidepressants]]
One of these is *ketamine*. It is an anesthetic that is sometimes abused as a [[hallucinogens|psychedelic]]

It blocks hyperactive [[Receptor Biology|receptors]] of [[glutamate]]

Ketamine can provide very quick relief from [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]], in as little as an hour
But this relief often dissipates within a week

Ketamine acts as an [[opioids|opioid]], which has raised concerns

However, Ketamine stimulates new [[synapse|synapses]], which helps with long-term change
