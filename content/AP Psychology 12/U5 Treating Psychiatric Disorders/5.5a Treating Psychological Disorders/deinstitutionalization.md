
>[!psych] deinstitutionalization
> the process, begun in the late twentieth century, of moving people with [[psychological disorder]]s out of institutional facilities

Since the 1950s, [[5.5d drug therapies|psychotropic drug]] therapies and community-based programs emptied most psychiatric hospitals. 
This deinstitutionalization left many with mental illness untreated

This has contributed to increased homelessness and incarceration
