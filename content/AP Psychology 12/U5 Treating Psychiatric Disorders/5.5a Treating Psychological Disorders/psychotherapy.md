>[!psych] psychotherapy
>treatment involving psychological techniques; consists of interactions between a trained therapist and someone seeking to overcome psychological difficulties or achieve personal growth

The Therapist may:
- explore early relationships
- encourage the client to adopt new ways of thinking
- coach the client to modify behavior

For those treated with "talk therapies", options are built on one or more of psychologies major disciplines: psychodynamic, humanistic, behavioral, and cognitive

Most psychotherapists describe their approach as *eclectic*
