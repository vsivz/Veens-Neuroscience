>[!psych] transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS)
> the application of repeated pulses of magnetic energy to the [[1.4b Brain Structure|brain]]; used to stimulate or suppress brain activity

This [[neural stimulation]] technique is performed on *wide-awake* patients in sessions over several weeks
Repeated pulses surging through a magnetic coil held close to the skull can stimulate or suppress activity in areas of the [[Cerebral Cortex|cortex]]
This produces no memory loss or other serious side effects, aside from possible headaches

![[Screenshot_20250114_233940 2.png]]

This therapy has had mixed results
Some studies found that it works for 30-40% of people [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]], although it is less effective than [[electroconvulsive therapy|ECT]]
It also reduces some [[5.4c Schizophrenia Spectrum Disorders|schizophrenia]] symptoms, including social apathy and memory loss

It is still unclear how it works
It may be because the stimulation energizes the left [[frontal lobes|frontal lobe]], which is relatively inactive during depression
repeated stimulation may cause [[neurons]] to form new circuits through [[long-term potentiation]]
It could also possibly just be the [[placebo effect]]
