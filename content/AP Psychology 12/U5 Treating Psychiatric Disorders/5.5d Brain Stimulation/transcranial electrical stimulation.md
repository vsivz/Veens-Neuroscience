*transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS)*  is a [[neural stimulation]] technique, involving administering a weak 1-2mA current to the scalp (compared to the ~800 mA from [[electroconvulsive therapy|ECT]])
Some skeptics argue that this current is too weak to penetrate the [[1.4b Brain Structure|brain]], but research suggests that tDCS is modestly effective for [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]]
