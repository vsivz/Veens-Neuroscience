>[!psych] psychosurgery
>surgery that removes or destroys [[1.4b Brain Structure|brain]] tissue to change behavior

Because its effects are *irreversible*, psychosurgery is the most drastic, and least-used [[biomedical therapy]] for changing behavior

![[lobotomy|clean no-t]]

**Modern Day:**
Today, precise, microscale psychosurgery may be used in extreme cases
If a patient suffers uncontrollable seizures, surgeons can deactivate the specific [[nerves|nerve]] clusters that cause or transmit the convulsions
MRI-guided precision surgery may also be done to cut the circuits, or to lesion brain tissue, involved in severe [[major depressive disorder]] and [[obsessive-compulsive disorder]]
But due to the irreversibility, this is often a last resort
