*Deep brain stimulation* is an experimental [[neural stimulation]] treatment for patients whose [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]] has resisted both [[5.5d drug therapies|drug therapy]] and [[electroconvulsive therapy|ECT]]
It pinpoints a neural hub that bridges the [[frontal lobes]] to the [[Limbic System]]
This is an area which is overactive in the brain of a depressed or temporarily sad person, and it typically calms when treated by ECT or [[antidepressants]]
DBS technology, which is sometimes used to treat Parkinson's tremors, can experimentally activate [[neurons]] that inhibit activity here

![[Screenshot_20250114_233940 3.png]]

Since 2003 neuroscientist Helen Mayberg used DBS to treat some 200 depressed patients. 
For some, DBS produces large and enduring reductions in depression.
