The [[4.5b Humanistic Theories of Personality|humanistic]] perspective emphasizes people's innate potential for *self-fulfillment*
These [[psychotherapy|therapies]] attempt to reduce the inner conflicts that interfere with natural growth and development, by helping clients discover new insights.
Because they share this goal, [[5.5a Psychodynamic Therapy|Psychodynamic]] and Humanistic therapies are often referred to as [[insight therapies]]

![[insight therapies|clean no-t]]

But there are 3 main differences between Humanistic and Psychodynamic:
- **Growth**: Humanistic therapists don't focus on illness, but helping people grow in *self-awareness* and *self-acceptance*
- **The Present**: Focuses on the present and future more than the past
- **The conscious mind**: Focuses on conscious thoughts rather than unconscious thoughtss

All of these themes are present in the widely used *person-centered therapy*
