**Ideomotor Effect**
A subconscious involuntary movement
You often accidentally move a little bit if you think about it

**Pattern-Seeking** 
Once it starts to spell something, you're going to try to guess, even subconsciously, because of our tendency to *pattern-seek*

Subconscious patterns will then affect the Ideomotor effect, which will then be more pronounced with more people, as everyone may subconsciously think of the same word.

**Experimental Results**
In a study in 2018, participants wore goggles with cameras that tracked their eye movement (Andersen, 2018). 

While the first letter is completely random, each letter after that becomes more *statistically significant*, as the eye-tracking showed them, with an increasing accuracy over time, guess the next letter.

**Revealing things that the participants "did not know"**
Some Ouija board users reported that the board communicated facts that they did not know

A reveals this may be due to subconscious memory (Rensink, 2012). 
Participants used a Ouija board to answer yes-or-no questions.
Participants were blindfolded and thought they were working with a partner- but that partner was a *confederate* who took their hand off the board.

When faced with a question they weren't confident in, participants guessed correctly more oftern when they thought they were just getting info from the Ouija board than when they were answering questions themselves consciously.

This is due to our *two-track mind*, with the *subconscious track* suggesting answers we don't consciously know.
