Recently, last Monday December 17th, I talked with my pediatrician, Dr. Jimenez.
He is generally okay with our current approach, although he made some changes.
- Firstly, he added an SSRI, Fluoxetine, to help deal with my anxiety
- Secondly, he wants to change the Guanfacine to Clonidine, but wants to wait until after I adjust to Fluoxetine. 

In general, other than some pretty mild initial gastrointestinal symptoms, I seem to be mostly adjusting well to Fluoxetine.
However there seems to be one key issue.

Normally, Dextroamphetamine works for me because of how dopaminergic it is.
Genetic testing has revealed that I am genetically predisposed to have a low basal dopamine.
So normally, without Dextroamphetamine, I have a harder time starting delayed-gratifying tasks, and instead will opt for instant-gratifying tasks, which was a major factor in my previous procrastination issues. 

However, Fluoxetine, by increasing Serotonin, can indirectly decrease dopamine activity. Genetic testing has shown that I am genetically predisposed to have very low basal Serotonin, likely meaning that I am much more sensitive to shifts in Serotonin levels, and I will have more pronounced dopaminergic suppression. 

Last Friday and Saturday, I decided not to take my second IR dextroamphetamine dose, to see what happens. 
On Friday, I took my first, SR dose in the morning. 
But in the evening, in the absence of dextroamphetamine, I didn't just have my usual issues with delayed-gratifying tasks. Rather, I felt depressed and anhedonic. There were several things that I had been excited to do previously, that I felt no interest in. I spent most of that night sitting down, doing nothing.
Saturday, saw less pronounced effects. I woke up much later, at 13:30, so I took my first medication much later, so its effects lasted much later. I did notice some milder depressive symptoms around 21:00, but it was not as severe as the previous day, as there were still likely some very mild dopaminergic effects from having dextroamphetamine in my system. 

So what I am seeing is that Dextroamphetamine is counteracting the depressive effects from Fluoxetine.
However, I have just started Fluoxetine, and the dopamine-suppression is likely only going to get worse. 
So I thought that it may be a possibly good idea to add a bit of Vyvanse, just so that I have a small steady amount of dextroamphetamine in my system after my SR and IR doses wear off to stop me from being depressed. 
I doubt that a small amount will be significantly risky. 20mg Vyvanse, for example, has the same Base Dextroamphetamine content as 8mg of my current Dextroamphetamine Sulfate, and it would be spread out across a much longer part of the day, so it would not be a significant difference to my current Dextroamphetamine, it would just be a small buffer against the depressive effects of Fluoxetine. 

Before this too, I have considered Vyvanse something that may be helpful for other problems.
My current IR dose of Dextroamphetamine is essential to helping me get my work done in the afternoon.
In particular, it helps me get started, and get into a sort of 'flow' state. Once I am in this state, even if interupted, I can easily get back to work. I can go for a 20 minute walk, come back, and easily pick up where I left off. However, once the medication wears off, while I can still continue to work in this flow state, I have other cognitive issues, that I think having a small amount of Vyvanse in my system would help with.
