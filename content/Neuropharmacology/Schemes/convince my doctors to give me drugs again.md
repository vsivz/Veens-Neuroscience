### Efficacy of current medication
- Guanfacine serves its own purpose, and it seems to be doing that
- Dextroamphetamine is, on its own, the single most effective ADHD med for my situation
- My primary issue is motivation, so I need a dopaminergic medication like Dextroamphetamine
- While [[Lisdexamfetamine]] is also just Dextroamphetamine, its onset is too slow to be effective on its own
- Adderall's Levoamphetamine component will just add symptoms without helping with my motivational issues
- Atomoxetine would be terrible, as it has 0 direct dopaminergic effect
- [[Methylphenidate|Methylphenidates]] do not have the wider area of effect that amphetamines do. Genetic data indicates that while I do have low dopamine levels, almost at the threshold for very low, (they gave me a basal dopamine level of -5 which was low, and a basal Serotonin level of -6 which was very low) but I have higher [[dopamine]] and [[norepinephrine]] activity in the [[Prefrontal cortex|PFC]]
- So, out of all the 10 medications in Canada, the only one that would work for my situation is Dextroamphetamine
