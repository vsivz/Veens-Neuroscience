This is an [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitor|SSRI]], as it increases [[serotonin]] by blocking [[SERT]]

It is one of the most potent and one of the most selective SSRIs. 
It also has some small effects increasing [[norepinephrine]] and [[acetylcholine]], but much less than its Serotonergic effect

It has a half life of ~21 hours, making it more likely to cause discontinuation symptoms than others SSRIs

Paroxetine has also been shown to be a potent inhibitor of the [[CYP2D6]] and [[CYP2B6]] enzymes

It tends to cause more side effects than other SSRIs, including weight gain, sexual side effects, and drowsiness. 
Among the SSRIs, Paroxetine has been shown to cause the most Sedation.

Its sold under brand name Paxil
