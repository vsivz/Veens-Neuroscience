Fluvoxamine is an [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitor|SSRI]], blocking [[SERT]] to increase [[serotonin]] levels

It is very selective for SERT compared to other receptors, with the sole exception of [[Sigma-1 Receptor]]s, which is why it is particularly effective for [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]].

Among the SSRIs, Fluvoxamine has been shown to cause the most gastrointestinal upset

It strongly inhibits [[CYP1A2]] and [[CYP2C19]], which can lead to possible interactions. 
Fluvoxamine has been observed to increase serum concentrations of [[mirtazapine]] 3- to 4-fold
