Sertraline is an antidepressant of the [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitor|SSRI]] class

###### Uses
Sertraline has been approved for:
- [[major depressive disorder]]
- [[obsessive-compulsive disorder]] (including children with OCD)
- [[posttraumatic stress disorder]]
- premenstrual dysphoric disorder
- [[Panic Disorder]]
- [[social anxiety disorder]]


The efficacy of Sertraline is similar to that of other SSRIs for depression
Sertraline has been shown to be consistently effective for [[persistent depressive disorder]]
A meta-analysis of 12 new-generation antidepressants showed that Sertraline and [[Escitalopram]] are the best in terms of efficacy and acceptability in the acute-phase treatment in adults with depression
In the melancholic subtype of depression, Sertraline is appears to work better than many SSRIs, and has comparable efficacy to [[tricyclic antidepressants]], which are usually more effective than most modern antidepressants but much less tolerated

Sertraline is effective for the treatment of OCD in adults, adolescents & children. The dosages required for effective OCD treatment are higher than the usual dosages for depression, and the onset of action is also slower for OCD than for depression. It has been shown to perform better than the TCA [[clomipramine]] (historically used as one of the most effective treatments)

A study showed Sertraline to be superior to placebo for the treatment of panic disorder. The response rate was independent of the dose. 
It decreased the frequency of panic attacks by 80% (vs. 45% for placebo)
It was equally effective for men and women, and for those with or without comorbid [[agoraphobia]]
The response rate was lower for patients with more severe panic
Starting treatment simultaneously with Sertraline and [[Clonazepam]], with subsequent gradual discontinuation of Clonazepam, may accelerate the response.
###### Administration + Related
It is commonly sold under brand name Zoloft

It has a half life of ~26 hours
Following a single oral dose, mean peak blood levels occur between 4.5 and 8.4 hours. 

For depression and teenage/adult OCD, the usual initial dose is 50 mg/day, and the maintenance dose is 50-200 mg/day. For children 6-12 with OCD, the initial dose is 25 mg/day.
For Panic, Post-Traumatic Stress, and Social Anxiety Disorders the initial dose is usually 25 mg/day, increased to 50 mg/day after a week. The maintenance dose is 50-200 mg/day. 

It is available as:
- Oral Capsules: 150, 200 mg
- Oral Solution: 20 mg/mL
- Oral Tablets: 25 mg, 50 mg, 100 mg

Over a dose-range of 150-200 mg, bioavailability is likely linearly dose-proportional

###### Mechanism and Neurobiology
increases [[serotonin]] levels by blocking [[SERT]]. 

It has a mild inhibition of [[DAT]], which increases synaptic [[dopamine]], especially at high doses, occupying ~20% of DAT receptors at doses of 200mg+
It can also act as a small [[agonist]] of [[Sigma-1 Receptor]]s, enhancing anxiolytic effects
However, these two interactions are far less significant than its effect on SERT, and will typically appear at higher doses. 

###### Pharmacokinetics
Sertraline is cleared through metabolism in the liver. It gets metabolized into *desmethylsertraline*, which also acts like an SSRI, although it's effects are significantly weaker. DMS then gets cleared in feces or urine. 
No individual CYP enzyme is thought to mediate more than 25-35% of Sertraline's metabolism to DMS. 


###### Side Effects
The most common side effects include:
- Nausea (up to 26%)
- Headache (up to 22%)
- Diarrhea (up to 20%)
- Dry mouth (up to 14%)
- Ejaculation failure (up to 14%)
- Drowsiness (up to 13%)
- Dizziness (up to 12%)
- Fatigue (up to 12%)

Other common side effects include:
- Appetite Loss
- Indigestion
- Increased Sweating
- Tremor 
- Agitation
- [[insomnia]]
- anxiety
- sexual problems

In children or adolescents, common side effects also include:
- unusual increase in muscle movements
- nosebleeds
- more frequent urination
- aggression
- possible slower growth rate

Serious side effects include [[Serotonin Syndrome]] and increased [[suicide|suicidal]] ideation

Studies showed no significant weight gain for Sertraline treatment for depression and OCD

In most cases, withdrawal symptoms of Sertraline are mild, short-lived and resolve without treatment
