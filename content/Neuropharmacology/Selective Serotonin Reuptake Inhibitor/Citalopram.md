Citalopram is an [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitor|SSRI]], which increases [[serotonin]] levels by blocking [[SERT]]

It has two enantiomers: S-(+)-Citalopram and R-(-)-Citalopram
Citalopram is a racemic mixture consisting of equal parts of each enantiomer
Only the S-(+) enantiomer has the desired antidepressant effect, so the S-(+) enantiomer is sold separately as [[Escitalopram]]

Compared to other SSRIs, citalopram is associated with a higher risk of abnormal heart rhythms.

It has a half life of ~35 hours

Its sold under the brand name Celexa
