SSRIs work by inhibiting the [[reuptake]] of [[serotonin]] in the brain, by binding to and blocking [[SERT]]
Serotonin is a neurotransmitter associated with mood regulation, [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]], [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]] and overall emotional balance.

SSRIs are approved for a variety of psychiatric conditions, including:
- [[major depressive disorder]]
- [[Generalized Anxiety Disorder]]
- [[obsessive-compulsive disorder]]
- [[Panic Disorder]]
- [[social anxiety disorder]]
- [[posttraumatic stress disorder]]

Common side effects include:
- Nausea, and other [[gastrointestinal tract|gastrointestinal]] issues
- [[insomnia]]
- sexual dysfunction

A rare but serious possible side effect is [[Serotonin Syndrome]], which can be fatal

There are 6 SSRIs:
- [[Fluoxetine]]
- [[Sertraline]]
- [[Citalopram]]
- [[Escitalopram]]
- [[Paroxetine]]
- [[Fluvoxamine]]
