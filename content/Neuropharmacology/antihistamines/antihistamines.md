Antihistamines are [[1.3b Drugs!|drugs]] that block the effects of [[histamine]], a chemical involved in allergic reactions, [[inflammation]], gastric acid secretion and [[neurotransmitter|neurotransmission]]

They are broadly categorized on their mechanism of action and clinical use. 

**[[H1-Antihistamines|H$\mathsf{_1}$-Antihistamines]]**
- They block [[histamine]] [[H1 receptors|H$\mathsf{_1}$ receptors]]
- *First-Generation*
	- Cross [[blood-brain barrier|BBB]] $\rightarrow$ cause sedation and drowsiness
	- [[Anticholinergics|Anticholinergic]] effects
- *Second-Generation*
	- Do not cross [[blood-brain barrier|BBB]] $\rightarrow$ minimal sedation
	- Longer duration of action 

**[[H2-Antihistamines|H$\mathsf{_2}$-Antihistamines]]**
- block histamine [[H2 Receptors|H$\mathsf{_2}$ receptors]] in the stomach, reducing gastric acid secretion
- do not cause sedation
