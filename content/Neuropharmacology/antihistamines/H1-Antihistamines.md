These are a kind of [[antihistamines|antihistamine]] that block [[histamine]] [[H1 receptors]], primarily involved in allergic reactions and inflammation. 

###### First-Generation H$\mathsf{_1}$-Antihistamines
- Cross the [[blood-brain barrier]] $\rightarrow$ cause [[sedative|sedation]] and drowsiness
- [[Anticholinergics|Anticholinergic]] Effects $\rightarrow$ dry mouth, dizziness, urinary retention, constipation
- Uses: Allergies, motion sickness, nausea, and insomnia

###### Second-Generation H$\mathsf{_1}$-Antihistamines
- Do not cross the BBB $\rightarrow$ minimal sedation
- Longer duration of action (once daily dosing)
- Less anticholinergic effects
- Uses: Seasonal allergies
