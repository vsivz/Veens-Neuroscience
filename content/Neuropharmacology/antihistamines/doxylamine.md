
Doxylamine is a first-generation [[H1-Antihistamines|H$\mathsf{_1}$-Antihistamine]], commonly used as a sedative-hypnotic for treating [[insomnia]]

### Mechanism 

###### Histamine
Doxylamine has [[antagonist]] and [[inverse agonist]] activity at [[histamine]] [[H1 receptors|H$\mathsf{_1}$ Receptors]]
By reducing histaminergic signaling, which increases cortical arousal, Doxylamine promotes sleep

###### Acetylcholine
To a noticeably lesser extent than with Histamine, Doxylamine has [[Anticholinergics|Anticholinergic]] activity at [[mAChRs]].
By reducing ACh, Cortical Arousal is reduced, but so is REM sleep. 
Furthermore, Anticholinergic activity is generally less sedative than antihistaminergic. 

Compared to [[diphenhydramine]], Doxylamine is more selective for Histamine, reducing cognitive side effects and increasing the potency of [[sedatives|sedation]]
