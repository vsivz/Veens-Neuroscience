H$\mathsf{_2}$-antihistamines are a kind of [[antihistamines|antihistamine]] that blocks [[H2 Receptors|H$\mathsf{_2}$ Receptors]] in the stomach, reducing gastric acid secretion. 
They have less [[Central Nervous System|CNS]] penetration, so they do not cause sedation. 

They have lost popularity due to PPIs (proton pump inhibitors) being more effective for acid suppression

Other than *Cimetidine*, other H$\mathsf{_2}$-antihistamines generally have minimal side effects

They tend to have a moderate half life, ranging from 2-12 hours.
