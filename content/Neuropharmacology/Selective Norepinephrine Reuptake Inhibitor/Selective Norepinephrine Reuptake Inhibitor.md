*Selective Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitors (sNRIs)* are a class of drugs that selectively inhibit the [[reuptake]] of [[norepinephrine]], by blocking its transporter, [[NET]]
This leads to increased levels of norepinephrine in the [[synapse|synaptic cleft]]

###### Usage
NRIs are primarily used for:
- [[ADHD]] - as a non-stimulant alternative
- [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]] - although this is rare, as norepinephrine reuptake alone typically isn't enough
- [[narcolepsy]]
- Hypotension, Orthostatic Hypotension
- Chronic Pain - in some cases, norepinephrine can have an analgesic effect

###### Examples
- [[Atomoxetine]]
- [[reboxetine]]
- [[viloxazine]]
