Adderall is a popular brand name of [[Mixed Amphetamine Salts]], used as a [[stimulants|stimulant]] [[ADHD meds|ADHD med]], and to treat [[narcolepsy]]

###### Dosage
It comes in the forms Adderall IR, Adderall XR & Mydayis (which are all from the same company, just refer to different release mechanisms)
All three of these are available generically.

Adderall XR contains a dual-release bead system, that releases the medication at two different times
Mydayis contains three drug delivery beads, that release medication immediately, mid-day & afternoon.

Adderall is available as:
- 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5, 15, 20, 30 mg Adderall IR Tablets
- 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30 mg Adderall XR capsules
- 12.5, 25, 37.5, 50 mg Mydayis Capsules


Dosage for ADHD:
- IR, Adults,[[Adolescence|adolescents]] & children (6+): 
	- Initial: 5 mg orally 1 or 2 times a day
	- Maintenance: Daily dose may be raised in 5 mg increments weekly until optimal
	- Maximum: Only in rare cases would it exceed 40 mg/day
- IR, Children (3-5):
	- Initial: 2.5 mg orally per day
	- Maintenance: Daily dose my be raised in 2.5 mg increments weekly until optimal
- XR, Adults:
	- Initial 20 mg/day
- XR, adolescents (13-17):
	- Initial: 10 mg/day
	- Maintenance: May be increased to 20 mg/day after a week if symptoms are not adequately controlled
	- Maximum: 30 mg/day
- XR, Children (6-12):
	- Initial: 5 or 10mg orally once a day
	- Maintenance: Daily dose may be raised in 5-10 mg increments at weekly intervals
	- Maximum: 30 mg/day
- Mydayis, Adults
	- Maintenance: Daily dose may be raised in 12.5 mg increments
	- Maximum: 50 mg
- Mydayis, Adolescents (13-17):
	- Initial: 12.5 mg
	- Maintenance: Daily dose may be raised in 12.5 mg increments
	- Maximum: 25 mg

Dosage for Narcolepsy:
- IR, adults, adolescents (12+)
	- Initial: 10 mg orally per day in divided doses
	- Maintenance: Daily dose may be raised in 10 mg increments at weekly intervals until optimal response is obtained
	- Maximum: Usually dosage doesn't exceed 60 mg per day in divided doses
- IR, children (6-11)
	- Initial: 5 mg orally per day in divided doses
	- Maintenance: Dose may be increased in 5 mg increments at weekly intervals until optimal

Note: Narcolepsy rarely occurs in children under 12. The Adderall IR FDA label recommends using [[Dextroamphetamine]] Sulfate instead.

For IR, when multiple doses are taken daily,  they should be taken 4-6 hours apart. 

###### Duration
Adderall IR generally lasts 4-6 hours
Adderall XR generally lasts 8-12 hours
Mydayis generally lasts 16 hours

###### Chemistry
30 mg of Adderall contains 18.8 mg of Base Mixed Amphetamine Salts, the rest is just inactive ingredients. 
This 18.8 mg is comprised of 14.1 mg Base Dextroamphetamine and 4.7 mg Base Levoamphetamine

###### Inactive Ingredients

- LACTITOL , MICROCRYSTALLINE CELLULOSE, COLLOIDAL SILICON DIOXIDE , MAGNESIUM STEARATE , AND OTHER
INGREDIENTS.
- 5 mg contains no color additives
- 7.5, 10 mg contain FD & C Blue #1
- 12.5, 15, 20, 30 mg contain FD & C Yellow #6
**Adderall XR**:
- gelatin capsules, hydroxypropyl methylcellulose, methacrylic acid copolymer, opadry beige, sugar spheres, talc, and triethyl citrate. Gelatin capsules contain edible inks, kosher gelatin, and titanium dioxide
- 5, 10, 15 mg contain FD&C Blue #2
- 20, 25, 30 mg contain red iron oxide & yellow iron oxide
**Mydayis**:
- hard gelatin capsules, ethylcellulose, hydroxypropyl methylcellulose, methacrylic acid copolymer, methyl acrylate, methyl methacrylate, methacrylic acid copolymer, opadry beige, sugar spheres, talc, and triethyl citrate. The gelatin capsules for all four strengths contain gelatin, titanium dioxide, yellow iron oxide, and edible inks.
- 12.5, 25 mg contain FD&C Blue #2
- 37.5 mg contains red iron oxide
- 50 mg contains D&C Red #28, D&C Red #33, FD&C Blue #1
