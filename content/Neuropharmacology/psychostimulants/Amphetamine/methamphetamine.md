
methamphetamine is an [[Amphetamine|Amphetamine]], [[stimulants|stimulating]] the [[Central Nervous System]], to increase [[dopamine]] especially, and [[norepinephrine]]


###### Uses
In the US, methamphetamine hydrochloride, sold as [[Desoxyn]] has been approved for treatment of [[ADHD]]

Methamphetamine is sometimes prescribed off-label for:
- Obesity
- [[narcolepsy]]
- idiopathic hypersomnia

###### Isomerism
Methamphetamine has two enantiomers:
- [[dextromethamphetamine]] - much more potent; contributes to the psychoactive effects; is in Desoxyn
- [[levomethamphetamine]] - much less potent; does not significantly contribute to psychoactive effects; has other uses, like as a nasal decongestant.

###### Pharmacokinetics
It is metabolized by [[CYP2D6]], [[dopamine beta-hydroxylase]], and several other enzymes metabolize methamphetamine

The bioavailability of methamphetamine varies by method of administration:
- 67% Orally
- 79% Intranasally
- 67-90% via Inhalation (Smoking)
- 100% intravenously

Following oral administration, methamphetamine is well-absorbed into the bloodstream; peak plasma concentrations are achieved in 3.13-6.3 hours

Among its metabolites, the ones with the most active effects are:
- Amphetamine
- 4-hydroxyamphentamine
- 4-hydroxynorephedrine
- 4-hydroxymethamphetamine
- norephedrine


###### Mechanism
The mechanism of methamphetamine is very similar to that of Amphetamine (See [[Amphetamine#Mechanism]])

There are a few differences:
It has an additional methyl group to amphetamine, which enhances its lipophilicity, allowing it to easily cross the blood-brain barrier

Methamphetamine is an [[agonist]] of [[alpha-2 receptors]] and [[sigma receptor|sigma receptors]] with a greater affinity for [[Sigma-1 Receptor|Sigma-1]] than [[Sigma-2 Receptor|Sigma-2]]
It also inhibits [[MAO-A]] and [[MAO-B]]

It is also better able  to bind to and interact with [[DAT]], and is transported more efficiently into the presynaptic [[neurons|neuron]]

It's half life gives it slower metabolism that typical amphetamine
