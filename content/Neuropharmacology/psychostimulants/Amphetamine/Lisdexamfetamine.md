Lisdexamfetamine is a popular [[ADHD meds|ADHD med]] and [[Amphetamine]], and is a [[prodrug]] of [[Dextroamphetamine]]

###### Usage
Lisdexamfetamine is commonly used for:
- [[ADHD]]
- moderate to severe [[binge-eating disorder]]

It may be used for other purposes like [[narcolepsy]] if deemed necessary
It may also be used as an [[antidepressants|antidepressant]] as an adjunct therapy

###### Administration + Related
It is most famously sold as Vyvanse, and also sometimes Elvanse

It is available as:
- 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 mg chewable tablets
- 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70 mg oral capsules

Dosage for ADHD (6+)
- Initial: 30 mg orally once a day in the morning
- Maintenance: 30-70 mg per day; may adjust dosage in increments of 10 mg or 20mg at approximately weekly intervals
- Maximum: 70 mg per day

Dosage for Binge-Eating Disorder (Adults):
- Initial: 30 mg orally once a day in the morning
- Maintenance: 50-70 mg per day; titrate dose in increments of 20 mg in approximately weekly intervals
- Maximum: 70 mg per day


###### Mechanism & Chemistry
Pharmaceutically, Lisdexamfetamine is usually sold as Lisdexamfetamine Dimeslyate
Lisdexamfetamine is an inactive prodrug of Dextroamphetamine. 
After oral ingestion, lisdexamfetamine is broken down by [[enzymes]] in red blood cells to form L-Lysine and Dextroamphetamine. 
The half-life of this conversion is roughly 1 hour, and is not affected by gastrointestinal pH.
Studies show a linear relationship between peak plasma concentration of dextroamphetamine and lisdexamfetamine doses up to lisdexamfetamine doses up to 250 mg. 

Effects generally start within 2 hours, and may last as long as 10-14 hours. 

Lisdexamfetamine, once converted into Dextroamphetamine, has the same [[Amphetamine#Mechanism|mechanism]], increasing [[dopamine]] and [[norepinephrine]], and the same [[Amphetamine#Pharmacokinetics|Pharmacokinetics]]

30 mg of Lisdexamfetamine Dimesylate (17.3 mg Base Lisdexamfetamine) contains ~8.9 mg of Base Dextroamphetamine.

###### Side Effects
It becomes Dextroamphetamine, which is one of the [[enantiomer|enantiomers]] of [[Amphetamine]], so it will have the same Amphetamine [[Amphetamine#Side Effects|side effects]], although they will be less peripheral and cardiovascular than an Amphetamine [[racemic mixture|racemate]] due to the absence of levoamphetamine.
