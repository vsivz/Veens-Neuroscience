Desoxyn is an [[ADHD meds|ADHD medication]], only available in the US. 

###### Usage & Administration
It is approved for the treatment of [[ADHD]] in children and adults (6+) in the US

It is available as a 5 mg tablet

The typical stating dose is 5 mg orally once or twice a day
