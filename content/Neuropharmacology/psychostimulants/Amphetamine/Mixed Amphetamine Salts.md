Mixed Amphetamine Salts typically refer to a specific formulation of [[Amphetamine]], (3:1 ratio of its D- and L-[[enantiomer|enantiomers]]), typically sold under brand name [[Adderall]], are popular [[stimulants|stimulant]] [[ADHD meds]]

###### Usage
Mixed Amphetamine Salts are commonly used to treat [[ADHD]] and [[narcolepsy]]

It is a very successful treatment for ADHD, and is a first-line treatment.
For narcolepsy, Amphetamines are less preferred relative to other [[stimulants]] and is considered a third-line treatment option

###### Chemistry
Mixed Amphetamine Salts are composed of equal parts [[Amphetamine|racemic amphetamine]] and [[Dextroamphetamine]],
This produces a (3:1) ratio between Dextroamphetamine and Levoamphetamine, the two enantiomers of [[Amphetamine]]

This ratio comes from a combination of 4 amphetamine salts:
*Dextroamphetamine Saccharate* - Provides a rapid onset, allowing the medication to start working quickly
*Dextroamphetamine Sulfate* - Also acts, relatively quickly, but is slightly longer lasting than Saccharate
*Amphetamine Aspartate Monohydrate* - [[racemic mixture|racemate]], containing both [[enantiomer|enantiomers]], balancing quick onset with moderate duration
*Amphetamine Sulfate* - Racemic amphetamine; Sulfate salts are slower to dissolve and have a longer duration of action

30 mg of Adderall contains 14.2 mg Base Dextroamphetamine and 4.5 mg Base Levoamphetamine

Because it is just Amphetamine, it has the same [[Amphetamine#Mechanism|Mechanism]] & [[Amphetamine#Pharmacokinetics|Pharmacokinetics]]


###### Side Effects
The [[Amphetamine#Side Effects|Side Effects]] are those of Amphetamine, as this is just an Amphetamine formulation.
This will generally have more peripheral and cardiovascular side effects than standalone Dextroamphetamine Sulfate due to the Levoamphetamine component.
