Biphentin is a [[stimulants|stimulant]] medication used primarily to treat [[ADHD]]

The base drug itself is just [[Methylphenidate]] HCl, and Biphentin just has a different release mechanism, so it has the Methylphenidate's 
 [[Methylphenidate#Usage|Usage]], [[Methylphenidate#Mechanism|Mechanism]], [[Methylphenidate#Pharmacokinetics and Interactions|Pharmacokinetics and interactions]] and [[Methylphenidate#Side Effects|Side Effects]] 

###### Administration
Biphentin comes in 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 & 80 mg controlled-release capsules
Capsules have a white body, and a cap with a color depending on strength: light turquoise blue, orange, yellow, blue violet, pink, light green, iron grey, reddish orange, for 10-80 mg respectively. 

The usual starting dose is 10-20 mg once daily in the morning, and may be titrated weekly in 10 mg/day increments
The maximum daily dose is 60 mg for children, and 80 mg for adults.
In some children, higher doses (maximum 1 mg/kg/day) may be necessary

If desired, the capsule may be opened, and the contents of the capsule (the medication) may be sprinkled onto apple sauce, ice cream or yogurt. Do not sprinkle in liquid. The entire mixture should be consumed within 30 minutes without chewing. 

The inactive ingredients include ammonio methacrylate copolymer type B, gelatin, hydroxypropyl methylcellulose, methacrylic acid and ethyl acetate copolymer, polyethylene glycol, sugar spheres, talc, titanium dioxide, triethyl citrate, in addition to dyes that differ based on the dosage of the pill.

###### Release Mechanism 
Biphentin uses a unique multilayer release (MLR) bead technology, using a mix of IR and ER beads, where 40% of the dose is provided as an immediate release, and 60% is provided through a gradual release.

Effects generally last around 10-12 hours

Biphentin has full bioavailability relative the same daily dose of two doses of instant-release methylphenidates
