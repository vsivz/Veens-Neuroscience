A Methylphenidate-based ADHD stimulant.

Comes in a scored tablet, and Wax Matrix Preparation

*Second-Line*

**Scored Tablet**
Allows the tablet to be split for customizable dosing
Generally lasts *3-4 hours*


**Wax Matrix Preparation**
Uses a wax matrix to slowly release methylphenidate over several hours
The wax matrix tends to be less consistend than newer extended-release technology
Generally lasts *~8 hours*
