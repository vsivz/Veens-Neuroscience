serdexmethylphenidate is a [[prodrug]] of [[dexmethylphenidate]]
It has a delayed onset of action, and prolonged duration compared to its parent

It is approved as one of the active ingredients in [[Azstarys]], for the treatment of [[ADHD]]

SDX is currently not packaged as a standalone drug. 

Following oral administration of SDX, D-TMP $\ce{T_{max}}$ is reached at about 8 hours post dose.

SDX is likely converted to D-TMP mainly in the lower [[gastrointestinal tract]]
