*serdexmethylphenidate/dexmethylphenidate*, sold under the brand name Azstarys, is a [[stimulants|stimulant]] used to treat [[ADHD]]

###### Administration
Azstarys (SDX/D-TMP) is available as:
- 26.1 mg/5.2 mg (SDX/D-TMP) oral capsules
- 39.2 mg/7.8 mg (SDX/D-TMP) oral capsules
- 52.3 mg/10.4 mg (SDX/D-TMP) oral capsules

Dosing:
- Children (6-12)
	- Recommended Starting Dose: 39.2 mg/7.8 mg
	- After one week, the dosage may be increased to 52.3 mg/10.4 mg or decreased to 26.1 mg/5.2 mg depending on tolerability
	- Maximum recommended dosage is 52.3 mg/10.4 mg
- Adolescents and Adults (13+)
	- Recommended Starting Dose: 39.2 mg/7.8 mg
	- After one week, increase to 52.3 mg/10.4 mg
	- Maximum recommended dosage is 52.3 mg/10.4 mg

Capsule should be taken 1 time each day in the morning, with or without food
Capsules may be swallowed whole
Otherwise, the capsule may be opened and the entire contents sprinkled onto 2 tablespoonfuls of applesauce, or into 50mL of water; This mixture must be consumed within 10 minutes of mixing

###### Chemistry and Pharmacokinetics
Azstarys is a combination of $\sim \frac{5}{6}$ [[serdexmethylphenidate]] and $\sim \frac{1}{6}$ [[dexmethylphenidate]]
SDX is a [[prodrug]] of D-TMP. MPH
So the combination allows some quickly onset effects from D-TMP and prolonged effects from SDX.

Following a single dose administration of Azstarys, the median time to reach $\ce{C_{max}}$ of SDX and D-TMP ($\ce{T_{max}}$) is about  2 hours under fasted conditions, and 4-4.5 hours in the presence of food. 

No clinically meaningful differences in the exposure of D-TMP were observed when administered after an overnight fast, with a high-fat, high-caloric meal, or sprinkled onto applesauce or water.

The mean elimination half-life from plasma of SDX and D-TMP were about 5.7 and 11.7 hours respectively

###### Side Effects
The most comment side effects include:
- decreased appetite
- nausea
- indigestion
- trouble sleeping/[[insomnia]]
- vomiting
- stomach pain
- weight loss
- dizziness
- mood swings
- increased blood pressure, heart rate
- [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]], irritability
