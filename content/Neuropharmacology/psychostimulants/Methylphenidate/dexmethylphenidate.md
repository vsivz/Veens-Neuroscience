Dexmethylphenidate is a [[ADHD meds|ADHD med]], often sold under the brand name [[Focalin]], 

###### Isomerism
Dexmethylphenidate is the most pharmacologically active [[stereoisomer]] of [[Methylphenidate]], and is thus more potent than the same dose of a traditional [[racemic mixture]] of Dexmethylphenidate/Levomethylphenidate, like [[Ritalin SR]]

Dexmethylphenidate is the RR [[enantiomer]]
In theory, D-TMP (d-threo-methylphenidate) can be approximated as twice the strength of the racemic product. 

###### Mechanism
It increases [[norepinephrine]] and [[dopamine]] levels
