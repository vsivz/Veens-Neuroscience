jPramipexole is a [[dopamine agonists|dopamine agonist]], mimicking the effects of [[dopamine]]

###### Usage
Only immediate-release pramipexole is approved to treat both [[Parkinson's Disease]] and Restless Legs Syndrome
Extended-release pramipexole is approved for just Parkinson's

It is prescribed off-label for other conditions, including [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]]

###### Administration
It is most commonly sold under the brand name Mirapex

Pramipexole is sold in the following forms:
- 0.125, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5 mg oral immediate release tablet
- 0.375, 0.75, 1.5, 2.25, 3, 3.75, 4.5 mg oral extended release tablets

For adults with Parkinson:
- Initial Dose: 0.125 mg taken 3 times/day (0.375 mg/day extended release)
- Titration: Increase by total 0.375 mg/day the first week, then 0.75 mg/day every week after until effective response
- Maximum Dosage: 4.5 mg/day (immediate or extended)
