dopamine agonists a kind of [[agonist]] [[1.3b Drugs!|drug]] that stimulate [[dopamine receptors]] in the [[1.4b Brain Structure|brain]], mimicking the effects of [[dopamine]], a key [[neurotransmitter]] involved in motivation, movement, reward and many other functions. 

They are primarily used to treat conditions like [[Parkinson's Disease]] or restless legs syndrome, and in some cases, [[psychological disorder|psychiatric disorders]]

![[dopamine#Dopamine System Overview|clean]]

###### Main Classes
- Ergot-derived dopamine agonists (older, higher risk of side effects):
	- [[Bromocriptine]] - Acts on [[D2 receptor|D2 receptors]] 
	- Pergolide, Cabergoline - stimulate both [[D1 receptor|D1]] and D2 receptors, but have been linked to heart valve fibrosis
- Non-ergot dopamine agonists (newer, better tolerated):
	- [[Pramipexole]] - High affinity for [[D3 receptors|D3]] > D2 > D4 receptors
	- [[Ropinirole]] - Acts mainly on D2 receptors
