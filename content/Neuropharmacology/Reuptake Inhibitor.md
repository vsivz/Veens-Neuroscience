*Reuptake Inhibitors (RIs)* are a wide class of [[1.3b Drugs!|drugs]] used to treat many [[psychological disorder|conditions]]

They work by inhibiting the [[reuptake]] of [[neurotransmitter|neurotransmitters]], allowing higher neurotransmitter levels in the [[synapse|synaptic cleft]]

Most known RIs affect [[serotonin]], [[norepinephrine]] & [[dopamine]]
