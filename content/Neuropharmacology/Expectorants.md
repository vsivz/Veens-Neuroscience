
#### Overview
Expectorants are a class of medications primarily used to aid in the clearance of mucus and phlegm from the airways, which can help alleviate symptoms of coughs and congestion. 

#### Mechanism
Expectorants increase the water content of the mucus, reducing its thickness. 
This is often achieved by drawing water into the airways. 

Once the mucus is less sticky, the natural cough reflex is better able to expel it from the lungs and bronchial tubes.
