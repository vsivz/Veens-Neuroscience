A *Serotonin Modulator and Stimulator (SMS)* is a type of [[antidepressants|antidepressant]] [[1.3b Drugs!|drug]] with multimodal action on the [[serotonin]] system

Similarly to [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitor|SSRIs]], they inhibit the [[reuptake]] of Serotonin, but unlike SSRIs, they also modulate one or more [[serotonin receptors|serotonin receptors]].

This term was created to describe the unique mechanism of [[vortioxetine]], and also apply to [[vilazodone]]

They may have benefit because the increased Serotonin activity from SSRIs increases activity in some receptors, which typically helps, but activation of [[serotonin autoreceptors]] leads to initially reduced Serotonin levels until these receptors desensitize. But [[agonist|agonism]] of these receptors speed up that process and [[antagonist|antagonism]] blocks the effect.
