TCAs were an older class of antidepressants, that have since been replaced by [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitor|SSRIs]] and [[Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitor|SNRIs]], among others

They [[antagonist|antagonize]] [[SERT]] and [[NET]], blocking the reuptake of [[serotonin]] and [[norepinephrine]]

However, unlike SSRIs and SNRIs, they are not very selective, interacting with many other receptor types, such as [[alpha-1 receptors]], [[histamine]] Receptors, and [[acetylcholine]] receptors. 
This is the cause behind many of their side effects. 

They do tend to have higher efficacy than most modern antidepressants, especially in severe and treatment-resistant cases, but are usually not preferred due to their side effect profile.
