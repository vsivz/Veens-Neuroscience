### Usage
It has been indicated for the treatment of hypertension
Extended-release formulations have been indicated for pediatric treatment of [[ADHD]]

### Administration + Related 
It is sold under brand names including Kapvay, Onyda XR & Catapres

- @ **Forms**
It is available in various forms, either as $\ce{Clonidine HCl}$ or as base Clonidine; $\ce{1 mg Clonidine HCl = 0.087 mg Base Clonidine}$:
- *Catapres* Instant-release tablets, $\ce{0.1, 0.2, 0.3 mg}$ ($\ce{0.1 mg Clonidine HCl}$)
- *Clonidine ER* Extended-release tablets, $\ce{0.17, 0.26 mg}$ of Base Clonidine
- *Onyda XR*, Extended-release oral suspension, $\ce{0.1 \frac{mg}{\text{mL}} Clonidine HCl }$
- *Kapvay* Extended-Release tablets, $\ce{0.1, 0.2 mg Clonidine HCl}$

- @ **Dosage**
Dosage for Hypertension in Adults, Catapres:
- Initial Dose: $\ce{0.1 mg\text{/}day}$ orally 2 times a day (morning and bedtime)
- Titration: Increments of $\ce{0.1 mg\text{/}day}$ may be made at weekly intervals to desired response
- Maintenance Dose: $\ce{0.2 - 0.6 mg\text{/}day}$ in divided doses
- Maximum Dose: $\ce{2.4 mg\text{/}day}$ in divided doses (Studies have indicated that after this there is no benefit, but in practice dosage rarely gets this high)
- Comments: Taking the larger portion of the oral daily dose at bedtime may minimize transient adjustment effects of dry mouth and drowsiness

Dosage for Hypertension in Adults, Clonidine ER:
- Initial Dose: $\ce{0.17 mg}$ once daily at bedtime
- Titration: Increments of $\ce{0.09 mg\text{/}day}$ at weekly intervals
- Maintenance dose: $\ce{0.17 - 0.52 mg\text{/}day}$

Dosage for ADHD, 6 years and older, Kapvay:
- Initial dose: $\ce{0.1 mg}$ orally at bedtime
- Titration: Increase in $\ce{0.1 mg\text{/}day}$ increments every 7 days until desired response; dosage should be administered twice daily (either split equally or with the higher portion given at bedtime)
- Maximum dose: $\ce{0.4 mg\text{/}day}$ in 2 divided doses
- Discontinuing: taper daily dose by no more than $\ce{0.1 mg\text{/}day}$ every 3-7 days

Dosage for ADHD, 6 years and older, Onyda XR:
- Initial Dose: $\ce{0.1 mg}$ orally once daily at bedtime
- Titration: Increased in increments of $\ce{0.1 mg/day}$ in weekly intervals
- Maximum recommended dose: $\ce{0.4 mg\text{/}day}$ once at bedtime
- Discontinuing: Taper dose in decrements of no more than $\ce{0.1 mg\text{/}day}$ every 3-7 days
