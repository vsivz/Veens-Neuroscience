Guanfacine is a *[[alpha-2 receptors|alpha-2a]] [[Adrenergic Receptors|adrenergic receptor]] agonist*
it primarily stimulates *postsynaptic alpha-2A adrenergic receptors* located primarily in the [[Prefrontal cortex]] (also few others like locus coeruleus, but Guanfacine primarily acts on PFC), by simulating [[norepinephrine]]
By doing so, it helps to filter out noise to sustain attention and control impulses. 
Activation of alpha-2A receptors lowers [[cAMP]]. high cAMP destabilizes PFC neurons, causing them to "drop out" of active networks. By lowering cAMP, Guanfacine promotes more consistent neural firing
By selectively targeting *alpha-2A* receptors, instead of other adrenergic receptors like alpha-2B, it reduces risk of widespread side effects like excessive sedation

Guanfacine also reduces norepinephrine and dopamine release in subcortical areas, including the [[amygdala]]. This results in a calming effect, helping with emotional regulation and [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]].
