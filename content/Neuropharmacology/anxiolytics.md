Anxiolytics are a kind of medication used to treat [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]] and related conditions.
They [[depressants|depress]] [[Central Nervous System]] activity
Some have been used in combination with [[psychotherapy]] to:
- enhance [[exposure therapies|exposure therapy]]'s [[extinction]] of learned fears
- relieve the symptoms of [[posttraumatic stress disorder|PTSD]] and [[obsessive-compulsive disorder]]

Some critics fear that antianxiety drugs may reduce symptoms without resolving the underlying problems, especially when used as an ongoing treatment. 

The main kinds include:
- [[Benzodiazepines]]
- Azapirones, like Buspirone
- Beta-Blockers
Most anxiolytics are also [[antidepressants]], due to similar underlying causes for both anxiety and [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]]
- [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitor]]
- [[Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitor]]
- [[tricyclic antidepressants]]
- [[Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors]]
- [[Atypical Antidepressants]]
	- [[Serotonin Modulator and Stimulator]]
	- SNDRIs
	- SARIs
	- NRIs
	- NDRIs
	- TeCAs
	- Atypical Antipsychotics
