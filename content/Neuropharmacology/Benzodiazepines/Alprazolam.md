Known more famously as **Xanax**

It is primarily used for [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety disorders]], especially [[Panic Disorder]] and [[Generalized Anxiety Disorder]]

Common side effects include:
- sleepiness
- [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]]
- suppressed emotions
- mild- to- severe decreases in motor skills
- decreased cognition and alertness

Quick onset of action, with effects peaking within 1-2 hours. 
Short half life, (~11-16 hours)
Its a short-acting BZD

It binds to the [[Benzodiazepines|Benzodiazepine]] site of GABA$_{A}$, potentiating [[GABA]]. Alprazolam shows high affinity for [[GABA-A Alpha-1 subunit|Alpha-1]], [[GABA-A Alpha-2 subunit|Alpha-2]], and [[GABA-A Alpha-3 subunit|Alpha-3]] units, contributing to a wide range of sedative and anxiolytic effects. 

It is primarily metabolized via [[CYP3A4]]
