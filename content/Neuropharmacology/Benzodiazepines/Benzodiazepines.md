Benzodiazepines (BZDs) are a class of [[5.5d drug therapies|psychotropic drug]] that are widely used due to their [[sedative]] and [[anxiolytics|anxiolytic]] properties, to treat conditions including [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]] and [[insomnia]]

They primarily act by potentiating [[GABA]] at the GABA$_{A}$ receptor, binding to [[allosteric sites|allosteric site]] 

This receptor is made up of 5-subunits. Most importantly are the 2 *Alpha* subunits. The type of this will impact what function this receptors will have.
- Type $\alpha 1$: Helps with sleep and sedation. It's also responsible for the forgetting effect.
- Type $\alpha 2$ and $\alpha 3$: Reduces anxiety and relaxes muscles
- Type $\alpha5$: Reduces memory and thinking ability

Different BZDs will have varying levels of selectivity for different kinds of these, impacting what effects they will have.
Newer drugs are more selective, allowing for more precise targeting of effects
