
This [[Benzodiazepines|Benzodiazepine]] is primarily used for seizures and [[Panic Disorder]] (including [[agoraphobia]])

Peak blood concentration levels are typically reached after 1-2 hours, although in some individuals it may take 4-8.
It is a long-acting BZD
It has a half-life of 19-60 hours.

Common side effects include:
- Sedation
- Euphoria
- Motor Impairment

At the GABA$_{A}$ receptor, it potentiates [[GABA]]. It has more affinity for [[GABA-A Alpha-2 subunit|Alpha-2]] and [[GABA-A Alpha-3 subunit|Alpha-3]] units, helping with its [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]]-reducing effects. It also has some affinity for [[GABA-A Alpha-1 subunit|Alpha-1]] receptors, giving it mild [[sedative]] properties. It has very mild [[GABA-A Alpha-5 subunit|Alpha-5]] affinity, so it has less cognitive side-effects than other BZDs

It is metabolized by cytochrome P450 enzymes, especially [[CYP3A4]]
