
This [[Benzodiazepines|Benzodiazepine]] is used primarily for [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]], seizures and [[alcohol use disorder|alcohol withdrawal]]

Common side effects include:
- Drowsiness
- Headache
- Muscle Weakness
- Trouble with balance or coordination

It is highly lipophilic, meaning it can very quickly cross the blood-brain-barrier
Its onset of action is 1-5 minutes of IV administration, and 15-30 minutes for IM administration
The half-life is typically ~30-56 hours

It binds to the BZD site on GABA$_{A}$ receptors, potentiating [[GABA]]
It has high affinity for [[GABA-A Alpha-1 subunit|Alpha-1]] and [[GABA-A Alpha-2 subunit|Alpha-2]] subunits, in addition to moderate affinity for [[GABA-A Alpha-3 subunit|Alpha-3]] and [[GABA-A Alpha-5 subunit|Alpha-5]], providing a wide range of effects. 

It its metabolized via a wide range of CYP enzymes
