###### Overview & Usage
Antidepressants are a kind of medication used to treat [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depressive disorders]], but also other conditions including:
- [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety disorders]]
- [[5.4a Obsessive-Compulsive and Related Disorders|Obsessive-Compulsive and Related Disorders]]
- [[posttraumatic stress disorder]]
- [[sleep disorders]]
- chronic pain. 

###### Main Kinds
- [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitor]]s - increase [[serotonin]], by blocking its [[reuptake]]
	- [[Fluoxetine]]
	- [[Fluvoxamine]]
	- [[Paroxetine]]
	- [[Sertraline]]
	- [[Citalopram]]
	- [[Escitalopram]]
	  
- [[Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitor]]s - increase serotonin and [[norepinephrine]], by blocking their reuptake
	- [[Venlafaxine]]
	- [[Desvenlafaxine]]
	- [[Duloxetine]]
	  
- [[tricyclic antidepressants]] - these are similar to SNRIs, blocking Serotonin and Norepinephrine reuptake, but are less  selective, and interact with other receptors, increasing side effects; they are not typically used unless other forms fail
  
- [[Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors]] - these inhibit the enzyme [[Monoamine Oxidase|MAO]], which is responsible for the breakdown of some [[neurotransmitter]]s, including serotonin, norepinephrine and [[dopamine]]; older generation antidepressant that is typically not used due to dietary restrictions
  
- [[Atypical Antidepressants]] - any other kind of antidepressant:
	- [[Serotonin Modulator and Stimulator]]s - increase serotonin by blocking reuptake, and also interacts with [[serotonin receptors]]
		- [[vortioxetine]]
		- [[vilazodone]]
	- SNDRIs - blocks the reuptake of Serotonin, Norepinephrine and Dopamine
	- SARIs - similar to SMSs, they specifically block serotonin reuptake, and [[antagonist|antagonize]] certain serotonin receptors
	- NRIs - block the reuptake of Norepinephrine
	- NDRIs - block the reuptake of Norepinephrine and Dopamine
	- TeCAs - similar to TCAs, they block the reuptake of various neurotransmitters, and are no longer frequently used
	- Atypical Antipsychotics
- Researchers are exploring the benefits of [[ketamine]] for short-acting depression relief
