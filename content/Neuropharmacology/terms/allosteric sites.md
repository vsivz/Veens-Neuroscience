Allosteric sites are regions on a protein, typically an enzyme or a receptor, that are distinct from the protein's active site/primary binding site

They are located a distance from the active site

Binding of an allosteric modulator can change protein's shape, affecting its activity
*Positive Allosteric Modulators* (PAMs) enhance the activity of the protein, increasing efficacy
*Negative Allosteric Modulators* (NAMs) decrease the activity of the protein
