L-theanine is an [[amino acid]] that is found in green tea, and is responsible for its relaxing effects

Theanine has been known to act antagonistically against paralysis induced by [[caffeine]]

Experiments showed that theanine induced generation of [[alpha waves]], which are associated with relaxation

###### Learning
It has been shown that administration of theanine has a significant effect on the release or reduction of [[neurotransmitter|neurotransmitters]]

Through this, theanine plays a positive role in learning. 

Theanine has been shown to cause a significant increase of [[dopamine]] in the [[striatum]]
Administration of Theanine seems to decrease [[serotonin]] levels, either through decreased synthesis/release or increased degredation
