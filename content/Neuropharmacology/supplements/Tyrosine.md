Tyrosine is a crucial [[amino acid]] that plays a vital role in the synthesis of several key [[neurotransmitter|neurotransmitters]]

###### Synthesis of [[Catecholamine|Catecholamines]]
*Biosynthesis of Tyrosine*
Tyrosine is synthesized from [[Phenylaline]] by the enzyme phenylaline hydroxylase (PAH)
$$\ce{\begin{align*}
Phenyla\text{line} + O_{2} + BH_{4} &\to Tyrosine + BH_{2} + H_{2}O
\end{align*}}$$
- This process primarily happens in the liver
- Uses tetrahydrobiopterin (BH$\mathsf{_{4}}$) as a cofactor. 


*Hydroxylation of Tyrosine → L-DOPA*
The enzyme [[tyrosine hydroxylase]] (TH) synthesizes [[L-DOPA]] from tyrosine
$$\ce{\begin{align*}
Tyrosine + O_{2} + BH_{4} &\to \text{L-DOPA} + H_{2}O + BH_{2}
\end{align*}}$$
- primarily occurs in the [[cytoplasm]] of catecholaminergic [[neurons]] and [[adrenal medulla]]
- uses BH$\mathsf{_{4}}$ as a cofactor again
- inhibited by [[dopamine]], [[norepinephrine]] & [[epinephrine]] via feedback inhibition
- activated by phorsphorylation via [[Protein Kinase A]] (PKA), [[cAMP]] & calcium-dependent kinases during [[stress]]


*Decarboxylation of L-DOPA → Dopamine* 
The enzyme Aromatic L-Amino Acid Decarboxylase (AADC) synthesizes Dopamine from L-DOPA
$$\ce{\begin{align*}
\text{L-DOPA} &\to Dopamine + CO_{2}
\end{align*}}$$
- Occurs in cytoplasm
- The cofactor is Pyridoxal Phosphate (PLP, Vitamin $\mathsf{B_{6}}$)
- AADC, and by extension this reaction, is not as tightly regulated as TH, so the reaction is dependent on L-DOPA availability


*Hydroxylation of Dopamine → Norepinephrine*
The enzyme [[dopamine beta-hydroxylase]] (DBH) synthesizes Norepinephrine from Dopamine
$$\ce{\begin{align*}
Dopamine + O_{2} + Ascorbate &\to Norepinephrine + Dehydoascorbate + H_{2}O
\end{align*}}$$
- Dopamine is actively transported into the synaptic vesicles of noradrenergic neurons via [[VMAT2]], where this reaction can then unfold
- The cofactors are Vitamin C (Ascorbate) and $\ce{Cu^2+}$ Ions
- This is increased by [[sympathetic nervous system]] activation
- This is stimulated by [[acetylcholine]] release in the adrenal medulla

*Methylation of Norepinephrine → Epinephrine*
The enzyme PNMT synthesizes Epinephrine from Norepinephrine
$$\ce{\begin{align*}
Norepinephrine + SAM &\to Epinephrine + \text{S-Adenosylhomocysteine}
\end{align*}}$$
- This happens in the cytoplasm, mainly in the adrenal medulla
- The cofactor is S-Adenosylmethionine (SAM)
- This is increased by [[cortisol]], which induces PNMT expression in the adrenal medulla
- Stimulated by stress and Sympathetic Nervous System activity
