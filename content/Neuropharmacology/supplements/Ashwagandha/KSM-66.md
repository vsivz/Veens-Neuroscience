KSM-66 is a popular, patented strain of [[Ashwagandha]] extracts. 

It uses the *root only*, aligning closely with traditional Ayurvedic practices, providing a balanced effect profile that includes cognitive and physical performance and enhancement. 

It is standardized to 5% [[Withanolides]]

Contains primarily traditional root-based Withanolides, (ex. [[Withaferin A]] at low concentrations)
