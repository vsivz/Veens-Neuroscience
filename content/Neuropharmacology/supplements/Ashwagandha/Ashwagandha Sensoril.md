
**Ashwagandha Sensoril** is a popular, patented stain of [[Ashwagandha]]

It is extracted from *both roots and leaves*, leading to a higher concentration (10% [[Withanolides]]) than most strains. 
Higher presence of specific bioactives like [[Withaferin A]], which yield stronger [[anxiolytics|anxiolytic]], [[sedatives|sedative]], [[cortisol]]-lowering and calming effects.
