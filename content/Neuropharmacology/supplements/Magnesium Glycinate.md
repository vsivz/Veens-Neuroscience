
### Overview
*Magnesium Glycinate* is a chelated form of [[magnesium]], in which magnesium is bound to [[Glycine]].
This chelation not only improves the absorption of Magnesium in the digestive tract, but also minimizes [[gastrointestinal tract|gastrointestinal]] discomfort, which is often seen with other Magnesium formulations. 

### Functions
Magnesium Glycinate supplementation will lead to increased levels of Magnesium and Glycine in the body, which will have each of their respective effects including:
*Magnesium*
- Modulation of [[NMDA receptor|NMDA receptors]], preventing excitotoxicity
- Modulates the activity of [[calcium]] and [[potassium]] channels. 
- [[cofactor]] for over 300 enzymatic reactions
*Glycine*
- In the [[Central Nervous System|CNS]], it functions as a inhibitory [[neurotransmitter]]
- Co-[[agonist]] at the [[NMDA receptor]]
