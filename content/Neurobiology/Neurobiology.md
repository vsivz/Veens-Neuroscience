title: Neurobiology
---

Neurobiology is the study of the [[Biology]] of the [[1.2 Overview of the Nervous System|Nervous System]], including the [[1.4b Brain Structure|brain]], [[spinal cord]] & [[Peripheral Nervous System]], and its structure, function & development

It focuses on understanding how [[neurons]] and their [[neural networks|networks]] work to:
- process and transmit information
- control bodily functions
- enable behavior, emotions & cognition

Key topics include:
- Neuronal anatomy - the study of neurons and their parts
- Neural communication - the transmission of signals through electric impulses and chemical [[neurotransmitter|neurotransmitters]]
- Brain function - how different brain regions control function
- [[neuroplasticity]] - the adaptation of the nervous system
- developmental neurobiology
