This encodes for the [[5-HT1A]] [[serotonin receptors|serotonin receptor]], which is a [[serotonin autoreceptors|serotonin autoreceptor]]. Variations in this gene can affect the efficacy of [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitor|SSRIs]], as the increased [[serotonin]] from SSRIs may be offset by increased activity in these receptors, which will reduce Serotonin levels. 

###### rs6295
For this polymorphism the C allele is associated with lower gene expression, while the G allele is associated with higher gene expression. 

###### rs10042486
For this polymorphism, some studies have shown the C allele is associated with lower gene expression, while the T allele is associated with higher gene expression.
