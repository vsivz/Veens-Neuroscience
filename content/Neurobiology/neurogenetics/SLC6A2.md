
This [[genes|gene]] encodes the [[NET]], which is responsible for the [[reuptake]] of [[norepinephrine]]


###### rs2242446
This polymorphism can be (T/C)

[A study](https://sci-hub.ru/https://doi.org/10.9758/cpn.2015.13.1.53) associated TT with higher vulnerability to lasting emotional impact from child abuse in people with [[major depressive disorder|MDD]]. 
Those who reported high impact from abuse had a 50-fold greater odds of carrying the TT genotype, but the small sample size (high impact: n=12; total: n=51) leaves high room for error. 

This polymorphism is located in the promoter region of the gene. The C allele is associated with lower NET activity. ([source](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnins.2018.01051/full))
