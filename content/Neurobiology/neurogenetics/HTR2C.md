

This encodes the [[5-HT2C]] [[serotonin]] [[heteroreceptors|heteroreceptor]]



###### rs3813929
This can either be C or T
The 'C' allele of this polymorphism has been consistently associated with reduced receptor activity. 
- This is because this allele is associated with skipping exon 5 during mRNA splicing
- skipping exon 5 leads to the production of a INI isoform, which is missing a portion of the third intracellular loop, which is important for signal transduction and receptor function

###### rs1414334
This can either be a C or a G
The 'C' allele has been associated with higher rates of [[tobacco|smoking]] in patients treated with [[atypical antipsychotics]]
The 5-HT$\mathsf{_{2C}}$ receptor reduces [[dopamine]] in the [[mesolimbic pathway]], which would reduce the rewarding effects of [[nicotine]]. This means that a likely conclusion is that the 'C' allele is associated with lower receptor activity as that would lead to the higher dopamine levels that make smoking more rewarding
