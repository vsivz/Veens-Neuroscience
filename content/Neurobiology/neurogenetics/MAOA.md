This encodes the [[MAO-A]] enzyme, which breaks down [[monoamine|monoamines]], especially [[norepinephrine]], [[serotonin]] & [[dopamine]]

###### rs1137070
The A or T allele for this gene is associated with higher MAO-A activity, leading to reduced dopamine levels compared to the C or G allele. 

###### rs6323
The G allele for this gene is associated with higher MAO-A activity

###### rs1465107
The A allele for this gene is associated with higher MAO-A activity
