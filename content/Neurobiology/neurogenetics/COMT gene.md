
This gene encodes for the [[COMT]] enzyme, which is responsible for breakdown of catecholamines, especially dopamine
##### rs4680
the rs4680 polymorphism can either be:
- GG: Both alleles support high enzyme activity
- GA: Intermediate enzyme activity between AA and GG, one allele contributes to high activity, one contributes to low activity  
- AA: Both alleles cause low enzyme activity

###### A (Met):
The A allele results in 3 to 4-fold decrease in COMT enzyme activity

Lower COMT enzymatic activity, therefore higher dopamine levels, especially in the [[Prefrontal cortex|PFC]]
- more exploratory
- lower pain threshold
- more vulnerable to [[stress]] and [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]]
- more efficient at processing information under most conditions
- this efficiency decreases under stress, because dopamine levels are too high for optimal performance - [[Yerkes-Dodson Law]]

###### G (Val):
Higher COMT enzymatic activity, therefore less dopamine levels, especially in the PFC
- less exploratory
- higher pain threshold
- less vulnerable to stress
- less efficient at processing information under most conditions

***
COMT is decreased by [[estrogens|estrogen]], such that overall COMT activity in the PFC and other tissues is about 30% lower in [[Male-Female Differences|women than men]], this translates to about 30% higher baseline dopamine levels there in woman then men.

Therefore, some have suggested that the A allele may be more beneficial for men, who can have dopamine levels closer to an optimal. 

***
I have an AA type. This maximum dopaminergic activity in the PFC may explain why I have stronger working memory and analytical skills in structured conditions, and a higher vulnerability to stress and anxiety.
