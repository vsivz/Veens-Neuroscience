This encodes an isoform of the enzyme [[tryptophan hydroxylase]], which is responsible for the rate-limiting step in the synthesis of [[serotonin]]
This is the isoform that is largely found in the [[1.4b Brain Structure|brain]], especially in the [[neurons]] of the [[ralphe nulclei]]

###### rs4570625
This polymorphism can either be G or A
It is located in a region that may control how much of the enzyme is made
The G allele has been associated with more mental health issues:
- A study has found that it is more often transmitted from parents to children affected by [[obsessive-compulsive disorder|OCD]] than would occur by chance, indicating that it is associated with the disorder
- In general it has been associated with a higher predisposition to [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]]
