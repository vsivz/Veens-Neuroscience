This gene encodes the [[D2 receptor]] which is a [[dopamine receptors|dopamine receptor]]
Variations in this gene may indicate [[dopamine]]-related [[psychological disorder|disorders]]

###### rs1800497
The minor (A) allele is associated with lower receptor density compared to the G allele

###### rs1799732
The C Del polymorphism is associated with lower receptor density compared to CC

###### rs1076560
The C/C type is the most common, followed by the A/C. The A/A is very rare, with its prevalence ranging from 2-20% in various populations. 
The A allele has been associated with a lower level of the short version of the D2 receptor (D2S) compared to D2L in brain regions such as the [[Caudate Putamen]] and the [[Prefrontal cortex]]
Individuals with an A allele tend to have less D2 signaling than those with C/C
