This encodes the [[MAO-B]] enzyme, which breaks down [[monoamine|monoamines]], especially [[dopamine]]

This gene is located on the [[X Chromosome]], so for each of its polymorphisms, (for example using A and G) men can only have AA or GG, while females can also have GG. 
###### rs1799836
The A allele for this polymorphism indicates higher MAO-B activity, which leads to reduced [[dopamine]] levels, compared to the G allele. 
This means:
- AA - High Activity
- AG - Intermediate Activity
- GG - Low Activity
