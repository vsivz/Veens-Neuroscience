>[!neuroanatomy] nucleus
>In [[neuroanatomy]], the term 'nucleus' (plural 'nuclei') refers to a well-defined cluster of [[neurons|neuronal cell bodies]] within the [[Central Nervous System|Central Nervous System.]] These groups of neurons are typically organized based on similar functions, connections or [[neurotransmitter|neurochemical]] properties, and they appear as discrete regions of gray matter in anatomical sections
