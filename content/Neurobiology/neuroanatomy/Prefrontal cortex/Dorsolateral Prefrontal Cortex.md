Located on the superior and lateral portions of the [[Prefrontal cortex]] - Top and sides of forehead, above eyebrows, but not in middle. 

- Responsible for Rational Decision Making
- It is responsible for the manipulation and organization of information in [[working memory]]
- It is also responsible for inhibitory control and cognitive flexibility
- It allocates attentional resources to tasks, aiding in focus and multitasking


It receives input:
- [[parietal lobes]] for spatial working memory and attention
- [[temporal lobes|temporal regions]] for memory and language integration
There are extensive connections with the [[hippocampus]]

In the dlPFC, [[dopamine]] is extremely tied to higher-level cognitive processes, especially working memory and executive function
Among all neurotransmitters, dopamine is particularly relevant for modulating the "signal-to-noise" ratio in cortical neurons, influencing how the dlPFC processes relevant vs. irrelevant information

It requires optimal, 'Goldilocks', levels of DA

Here, [[NET]] and [[COMT]] are primarily responsible for [[reuptake]] and degradation of DA

[[norepinephrine]] levels are also important, especially for sustained attention
In higher states of stress, NE can shift dlPFC function toward more 'automated' or emotional responses, while optimal levels help the dlPFC maintain top-down control
