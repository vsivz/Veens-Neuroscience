Located on the medial wall of the [[Prefrontal cortex|PFC]], right above the Corpus Callosum

Functions:
- Conflict Monitoring: Detects conflicts between competing choices and signals adjustment
- Error detection: plays a role in recognizing and responding to errors
- Motivation and Drive
- Emotional Regulation

Dense connections with the motor system for response adjustments
