Located on the medial wall of the [[Prefrontal cortex|PFC]]

Functions:
- Social cognition: essential for empathy, [[Theory of Mind]]
- resolving cognitive conflicts and mediating goal-directed behavior
- Emotional regulation: Works with the [[Angular Cingulate Cortex|ACC]] to evaluate emotional conflicts and maintain focus on goals 
- Self-referential thinking

Strongly linked to regions involved in cognitive control, including the [[Dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex|dlPFC]] and the [[parietal lobes]]
links with the ACC for conflict monitoring. 

Dysfunction is seen in [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]] (excessive [[rumination]]), [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety disorders]], and [[autism spectrum disorder]]
