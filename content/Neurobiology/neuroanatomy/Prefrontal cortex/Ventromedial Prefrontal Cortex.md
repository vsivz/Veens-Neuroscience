Located on the Ventral and medial surfaces of the [[Prefrontal cortex|PFC]], in the lower middle of forehead. 

Functions:
- Emotional regulation: regulates emotions by modulating the [[amygdala]]
- Interprets emotional context
- The sense of "when" events occurred relies on interactions between the [[hippocampus]] and the vmPFC
- Value-based choices: assesses subjective value during desicion making
- self-referential thinking
- autonomic regulation: influences physiological responses, related to emotional stress

Extenstive connections with [[amygdala]] for emotional processing
Connected with the [[hippocampus]] (for memory integration)
connected with reward systems
