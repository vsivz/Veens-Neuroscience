Located on the inferior and lateral portoins of the [[Prefrontal cortex|PFC]] - on the lower sides of forehead, near temples. Just below the [[Dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex|DLPFC]]

Functions:
- Attention Control - Directs attention and suppresses irrelevant stimuli
- Response Selection 
- Emotional Regulation: Interacts with the [[amygdala]] and other [[Limbic System|limbic]] structures; Cognitive reappraisal and conscious regulation of emotions. 

Connected to posterior sensory regions for integrating sensory inputs
