The Prefrontal Cortex is located at the front of the [[frontal lobes]]. 
It is integral to [[Executive Funtions|executive functioning]]
It plays a crucial role in [[working memory]]
Also contributes to attention regulation, emotional control and personality expression. 

It is generally split into the [[Lateral Prefrontal Cortex]], [[Medial Prefrontal Cortex (outer)]], and [[OFC]]
