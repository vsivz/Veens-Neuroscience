Located on the ventral surface of the [[Prefrontal cortex|PFC]], above the orbital sockets of the eyes - this region is on the underside of the brain

In general, the OFC is the punishment vs. reward center in the PFC, and uses punishments and rewards to influence other functions

Functions:
- Reward and punishment processing: The OFC evaluates the relative value of rewards and punishments to guide decision making
- Social Behavior: assessess social rewards and punishments 
- Emotional Regulation: guides desicion making based on emotional rewards and punishments
- Sensory integration: combines sensory inputs to assess complex situations

Strong connections with the [[amygdala]] and ventral [[striatum]]
