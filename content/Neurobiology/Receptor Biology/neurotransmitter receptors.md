The receptor is a protein structure located on the surface or inside of a cell that binds to some [[neurotransmitter]]

A receptor will have a specific shape and chemical properties that allow it to interact with only one, or very few, neurotransmitters

When a neurotransmitter binds to the receptor, it triggers a response in the cell.

The binding of a neurotransmitter to its receptor typically mediates the neurotransmitter's effects in the nervous system or body:
- Excitatory effects: Makes the target cell more likely to fire an [[action potential]]
- Inhibitory effects: Makes the target cell less likely to fire an action potential
- Modulatory effects: Changes the overall tone or activity of a [[neural networks|neural network]]; can affect strength of connections; timing of neuronal firing; long-term changes in network behavior
