This subunit of the GABA$_{A}$ Receptor modulates [[GABA]] effects

It is widespread, particularly in the [[Thalamus]], [[Cerebellum]], and [[Cerebral Cortex]]

It mediates the sedative, hypnotic and anticonvulsant effects of [[Benzodiazepines]], and plays a role in the amnesia
