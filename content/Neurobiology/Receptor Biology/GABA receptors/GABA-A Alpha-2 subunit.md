This subunit of the GABA$_{A}$ Receptor modulates [[GABA]] effects

Its highly expressed in [[Limbic System]] Structures like the [[amygdala]] and [[hippocampus]], and also the [[Cerebral Cortex]]

It is primarily associated with the [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]]-relieving and muscle-relaxant effects of [[Benzodiazepines]], and plays a minor role in anticonvulsant effects
