This subunit of the GABA$_{A}$ Receptor modulates [[GABA]] effects

It is found in the [[brainstem]], [[spinal cord]], and certain [[Limbic System]] areas

It is similar to the [[GABA-A Alpha-2 subunit|Alpha-2]] receptors, reducing [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]] and relaxing muscles.
Is anxiety-reducing effects, however, are less prominent than [[GABA-A Alpha-2 subunit|Alpha-2]] receptors, and its more focused on muscle relaxation.
