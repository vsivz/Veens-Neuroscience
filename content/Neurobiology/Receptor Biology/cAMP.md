cAMP is a second messenger that carries intracellular messages
it mediates the action of various [[neurotransmitter]]s 
cAMP can influence the nucleus of a [[neurons|neuron]], turning certain [[genes]] on and off. This helps the cell adapt and change, which is essential for learning and memory.
It is primarily relevant to [[G-protein-coupled receptors|GPCRs]]

Fundamentally, cAMP takes the message from certain neurotransmitters to other relevant parts of the cell that need to respond.

In general, increasing cAMP means increasing activity, making cells more active
