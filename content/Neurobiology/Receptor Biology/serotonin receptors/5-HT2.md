These [[serotonin receptors]] are [[G-protein-coupled receptors|GPCRs]]
They are generally excitatory, they increase intracellular calcium levels

These have 3 subtypes:
- [[5-HT2A]]
- [[5-HT2B]]
- [[5-HT2C]]

Their roles include:
- Hallucination (5-HT2A)
- vascular tone
- appetite
- mood regulation

They are located in:
- [[Central Nervous System|CNS]]
- [[cardiovascular system]]
- [[gastrointestinal tract]]
