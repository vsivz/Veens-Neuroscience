These are a class of [[serotonin receptors]] that are unique in that they are [[Ionotropic Receptors|ligand-gated ion channels]], unlike all other [[serotonin]] receptors which are [[G-protein-coupled receptors|GPCRs]].

Their main roles are:
- Nausea and Vomiting
- [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]]
- [[gastrointestinal tract|gastrointestinal]] function

They are located in:
- [[Central Nervous System|CNS]]
- [[Peripheral Nervous System]]
- GI tract
