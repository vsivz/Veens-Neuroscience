Serotonin autoreceptors are a subtype of [[serotonin receptors]] that act as a *feedback mechanism* to regulate [[serotonin]] levels

Activation lowers Serotonin levels

They are located on presynaptic [[neurons]]

The existence of these are the major factor prolonging the time it takes for [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitor|SSRIs]] to reach therapeutic effect, as this effect can only be reached after desensitization. That is why newer antidepressant drugs, like [[Serotonin Modulator and Stimulator|SMS]]s will [[agonist|agonize]] these receptors to try and desensitize them quicker.
