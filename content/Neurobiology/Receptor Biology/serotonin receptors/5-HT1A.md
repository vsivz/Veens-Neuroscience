These are a subtype of the [[5-HT1]] [[serotonin receptors]]

It is a [[G-protein-coupled receptors|GPCR]], and acts as a [[serotonin autoreceptors|serotonin autoreceptor]] presynaptically

###### Presynaptic Autoreceptor Role

In the [[Dorsal Ralphe Nucleus]], a key [[serotonin]]-producing region, it acts as an autoreceptor 

When activated by serotonin, it inhibits further serotonin release via negative feedback

###### Postsynaptic Receptor Role

It regulates neuronal excitability and signaling, influencing processes such as mood, [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]] and cognition
This includes decreasing [[cAMP]] levels

In the [[hypothalamus]], particularly in regions influencing [[HPA axis]] activity, postsynaptic 5-HT1A receptors modulate downstream excitability of hypothalamic circuits. 

In the [[amygdala]] and other [[Limbic System|Limbic Structures]], they inhibit the excitability of [[neurons]] that drive fear responses

In the [[hippocampus]] it regulates [[long-term potentiation]] and [[long-term depression]] and enhances network stability by reducing excessive excitatory activity, ensuring proper signal-to-noise ratios

They are also located in the [[Prefrontal cortex]], preventing hyperactivity to maintain stability
