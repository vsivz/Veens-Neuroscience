[[serotonin]] Receptor
G protein-coupled receptor

##### Location
these receptors are abundant in the [[Prefrontal cortex]], regulating cognition, decision-making, and perception
also found in subcortical regions, like the [[amygdala]], [[hippocampus]] and [[Thalamus]]


##### Serotonin-Dopamine Interplay
They play a critical role in the interaction between serotonin and [[dopamine]] systems

**In the PFC**
They modulate [[glutamate|glutamatergic]] signaling in the prefrontal cortex: 
Activation of 5-HT2A receptors on glutamatergic neurons stimulates glutamate release, which in turn affects dopamine release.

**In the Mesolimbic pathway**
It is expressed on dopaminergic neurons in the [[midbrain]], including the [[ventral tegmental area]], and [[substantia nigra]]
This can increase dopamine release in the [[Nucleus Accumbens]], increasing reward processing and possibly [[psychosis]], and especially motor function
