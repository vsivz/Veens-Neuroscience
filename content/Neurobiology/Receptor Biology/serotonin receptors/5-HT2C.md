[[serotonin]] receptors

##### Effects on Dopamine
In the [[ventral tegmental area]] and [[striatum]] it acts on [[GABA|GABAergic]] [[interneurons]], which inhibit [[dopamine|dopaminergic]] neurons
This reduces dopamine release in reward centers, increasing [[anhedonia]].

It also has the same effects in the [[Prefrontal cortex|PFC]], reducing dopamine there. This can have some impairment of cognitive functions and can increase functional anhedonia. However the [[5-HT2A]] receptor has more significant effect here. 

#### Effects on Appetite
In the hypothalamus, this receptor is expressed on [[POMC]] neurons, and its activation releases peptides that suppress appetite
This is one of its *most significant effects*. 

#### Effects on Stress
In the [[amygdala]], it increases GABA, and decreases [[glutamate]], leading to reduced excitability of amygdala circuits, dampening fear and [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]] responses.
This is another *significant* effect.

#### Effects on Memory
In the [[hippocampus]], it decreases glutamate and increases GABA. 
This can impair memory formation, but enhance stress resilience.

#### Effects on other neurotransmitters
In the [[Dorsal Ralphe Nucleus]], it activates GABAergic interneurons which inhibit serotonergic neurons, reducing serotonin.
In the [[locus coeruleus]], it activates GABAergic interneurons, which inhibit noradrenergic neurons in the LC, reducing [[norepinephrine]].
