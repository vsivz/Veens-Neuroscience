title: Adrenergic Receptor
---
These are [[Receptor Biology|receptors]] located on the surface of certain cells in the body that respond to [[epinephrine]] and [[norepinephrine]]
These receptors are part of the [[sympathetic nervous system]]

There are two main types:
Alpha receptors:
- [[alpha-1 receptors]] 
- [[alpha-2 receptors]]

Beta receptors:
- [[beta-1 receptors]]
- [[beta-2 receptors]]
- beta-3 receptors
