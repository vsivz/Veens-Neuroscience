Effects:
- decrease the release of [[norepinephrine]] via negative feedback
- causes sedation and reduces pain in the [[Central Nervous System]]

When norepinephrine is released from a presynaptic nerve, it binds to $\alpha2$ receptors
Activation of these receptors inhibits further release of norepinephrine

With [[ADHD]]:
Activation of $\alpha 2a$ receptors:
- strengthens the signaling between neurons in the [[Prefrontal cortex]], improving attention and [[working memory]]
- inhibits distractibility by filtering out irrelevant information
- reduces hyperactivity by regulating impulsive neural firing
