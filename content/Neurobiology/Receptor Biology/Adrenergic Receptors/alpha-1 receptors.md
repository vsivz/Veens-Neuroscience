---
aliases:
  - alpha-1 adrenergic receptors
---

This is a kind of [[Adrenergic Receptors]], so it responds to [[norepinephrine]] and [[epinephrine]]

Effects:
- Vasoconstriction of blood vessels in the skin, gastrointestinal tract, and kidneys
- Contraction of smooth muscles, such as in the bladder sphincter
