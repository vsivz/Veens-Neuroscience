This is a [[dopamine receptors|dopamine receptor]], so it gets activated by [[dopamine]]

They are [[G-protein-coupled receptors|GPCRs]] of the family of [[D2-like receptors]]


###### Location
D$\mathsf{_{4}}$ receptors are prominently expressed in the [[Prefrontal cortex]], which is a region critical for [[Executive Functions]]
Beyond the PFC, they are found in [[Limbic System|limbic system]], although at much lower density compared to other dopamine receptors
They are also highly expressed in the retina and by the pinealocytes of the pineal gland


###### Circadian Rhythm
D$\mathsf{_{4}}$ receptors have been shown to play a fundamental role in the circadian adrenergic control of [[melatonin]] synthesis by pinealocytes
