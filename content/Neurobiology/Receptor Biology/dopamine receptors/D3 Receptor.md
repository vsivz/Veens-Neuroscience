This is a [[dopamine receptors|dopamine receptor]], so it gets activated by [[dopamine]]

 They are [[G-protein-coupled receptors|GPCRs]] of the family of [[D2-like receptors]]

Compared to other dopamine receptors, they have a higher affinity for dopamine, so they can be triggered even by low levels of dopamine. 

###### Location 
Unlike [[D2 receptor|D2 receptors]] that are widely distributed, D$\mathsf{_{3}}$ receptors are predominantly found in [[Limbic System|limbic areas]]
- [[Nucleus Accumbens]]
- [[Olfactory tubercle]] and Islands of Calleja
- Other Limbic Areas

###### Autoreceptor Function
In some [[neurons]] D$\mathsf{_{3}}$ receptors act as autoreceptors. When activated they help regulate the amount of dopamine released into [[synapse|synapses]], ensuring optimal signaling levels
This is essential for preventing overstimulation of downstream circuits

###### Reward & Motivation
Because they are much more sensitive to dopamine, D$\mathsf{_{3}}$ receptors are able to fine-tune dopaminergic signaling in response to modest changes in dopamine concentration, which is important for adjusting the motivational "value" or incentive salience of a [[stimulus]]
D$\mathsf{_{2}}$ Receptors generally require higher concentrations of dopamine for activation, making them more responsive during periods of strong dopaminergic release, such as a highly rewarding stimulus, but D$\mathsf{_{3}}$ are more useful in the majority of less exciting situations
