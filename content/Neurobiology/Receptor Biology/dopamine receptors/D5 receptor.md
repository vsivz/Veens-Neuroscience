This is a [[dopamine receptors|dopamine receptor]], so it gets activated by [[dopamine]]

 They are [[G-protein-coupled receptors|GPCRs]] of the family of [[D1-like receptors]], and they are expressed at lower levels than their [[D1 receptor]] counterparts

###### Memory
D$\mathsf{_{5}}$ receptors contribute to the mechanisms underlying [[long-term potentiation]] in key memory circuits
In the [[hippocampus]] they are are implicated in a process that is fundamental to encoding new memories
In the [[amygdala]], D$\mathsf{_{5}}$ help consolidate fear memories by coordinating with other [[Receptor Biology|receptors]]

###### Persistent Activity
The inherent high constitutive activity of D$\mathsf{_{5}}$ receptors means they may contribute a baseline level of [[cAMP]] signaling even without dopamine binding.
This persistent activity may be crucial for maintaining neuronal homeostasis, and ensuring that circuits remain resonsive
