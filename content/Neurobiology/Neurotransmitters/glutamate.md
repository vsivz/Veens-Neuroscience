glutamate is an [[amino acid]] and is the brain's primary excitatory [[neurotransmitter]]

It is involved in several key processes:
- Synaptic Transmission: facilitates the majority of fast excitatory synaptic communication
- [[neuroplasticity]]: essential for synaptic plasticity, including [[long-term potentiation]] and [[long-term depression]]
- Development: plays a role in brain development, particularly [[neurogenesis]] and [[synaptogenesis]]

Glutamate exerts its effects by binding to Glutamate Receptors, which can be Ionotropic or GPCRs
