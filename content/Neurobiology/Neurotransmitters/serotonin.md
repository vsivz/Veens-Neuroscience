A [[neurotransmitter]] involved in mood, anxiety, sleep, cognition and gut motility

Its chemical name is 5-HT

Around 90% is found in the gut
Around 8% is found in platelets and blood
Only 1-2% is found in the [[Central Nervous System|CNS]]

Exerts its effects through seven receptor families: ($5\text{-HT}_{1}$ to $5\text{-HT}_{7}$ )
