Gamma-Aminobutyric Acid is the primary inhibitory [[neurotransmitter]] in the [[Central Nervous System]] of mammals

GABA is synthesized from [[glutamate]]

Main functions:
- Inhibitory function: reduces neuronal excitability
- Neuronal plasticity: Modulates synaptic plasticity
- Neurodevelopment: during early brain development, GABA can act as an excitatory neurotransmitter
- Calming Effect: Reduces [[stress]] and [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]] by dampening neural activity
- Sleep regulation: Promotes sleep by inhibiting wake-promoting circuits
- Muscle relaxation
- Cognition and Mood
