The Norepinephrine Transporter

NET is a protein that [[reuptake|reuptakes]] [[norepinephrine]] from the [[synapse|synaptic cleft]]
It is primarily found on the presynaptic nerve terminals of noradrenergic [[neurons]]

Dysfunction in NET activity is implicated in [[5.4b Depressive Disorders|depression]] and [[ADHD]]

It is abundant in the [[Prefrontal cortex]], where it clears norepinephrine
Because [[DAT]] is so sparse in the PFC, NET is also the primary mechanism clearing [[dopamine]] from the PFC
