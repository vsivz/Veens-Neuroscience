This enzyme (catechol-O-methyltransferase) is the protein product of the [[COMT gene]]

It plays a role in the metabolism of catecholamines, including [[dopamine]], [[norepinephrine]] and [[epinephrine]]


COMT is particularly important in the [[Prefrontal cortex]], because [[DAT]] is less active there, so it is primarily responsible for clearing dopamine there
there, [[NET]] is primarily responsible for clearing NE, so it has less effect on noradrenergic activity

NET also clears DA there, and COMT plays a secondary effect.
