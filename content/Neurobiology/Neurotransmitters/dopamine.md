- Reward and Pleasure - Central to the brain's reward system

###### Dopamine System Overview
Dopamine is synthesized in dopaminergic [[neurons]] located primarily in:
- [[substantia nigra]] pars compacta (SNc) - important for motor control
- [[ventral tegmental area]] (VTA) - associated with reward and motivation
- [[hypothalamus]]

Dopaminergic neurons project to different brain regions, forming key pathways:
- [[nigrostriatal pathway]] - movement control
- [[mesolimbic pathway]] - reward and pleasure
- [[mesocortical pathway]] - cognition and executive function
- tuberoinfundibular pathway - prolactin regulation

Dopamine exerts its effects through dopamine receptors, which are divided into two main families:
- [[D1-like receptors]] - increase [[cAMP]]
- [[D2-like receptors]] - decrease cAMP
