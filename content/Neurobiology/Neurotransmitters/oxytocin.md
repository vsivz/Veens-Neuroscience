
oxytocin is a [[hormones|hormone]] primarily produced in the [[hypothalamus]] and secreted by the [[pituitary gland]]

It plays a role in social bonding, reproduction, childbirth and various other processes

Functions
- stimulates contractions in labor
- promotes milk flow while breastfeeding
- facilitates bonding
- reduces [[stress]] and [[5.4a Anxiety Disorders|anxiety]] in social contexts
- Acts as a stress buffer by decreasing [[cortisol]] levels
