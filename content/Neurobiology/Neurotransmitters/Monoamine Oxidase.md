MAO is an enzyme crucial for the metabolism of various [[neurotransmitter]]s

There are two main isoforms of MAO:
###### MAO-A
Preferentially metabolizes [[serotonin]] and [[norepinephrine]], [[epinephrine]] and [[dopamine]]
Highly expressed in the [[1.4b Brain Structure|brain]], [[liver]], [[gastrointestinal tract]], [[placenta]]

###### MAO-B
Preferentially metabolizes [[dopamine]] and phenylethylamine
Predominantly found in the brain and platelets
