Norepinephrine (NE), also known as noradrenaline, is a [[neurotransmitter]] and [[hormones|hormone]]
It exerts its affect through [[Adrenergic Receptors]]

It acts as a hormone during the "fight-or-flight" response:
- increased heart rate
- vasoconstriction
- bronchodilation
- energy mobilization

In the [[Central Nervous System]]:
- arousal and alertness
- attention and focus
- mood regulation
- stress response
- memory consolidation
